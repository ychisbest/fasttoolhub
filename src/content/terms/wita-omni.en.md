It lets a robot hold natural spoken conversations with people while taking in what it sees and hears.\n\nSource: [@RobotDollLab on X](https://x.com/RobotDollLab/status/2115694119464935614) · Event lineup: [AGIBOT Hong Kong](https://x.com/AGIBOTHongkong/status/2115701591831728443)"
explain: "## Key points\n\n1. **End-to-end and full-modal**: one model handles listening, understanding and speaking (plus visual context), instead of chaining separate speech recognition, LLM and text-to-speech steps.\n2. **Fast replies**: response latency as low as **400 ms**, about **600 ms** on average, close to human turn-taking.\n3. **Languages**: **100+ languages and 29 dialects**.\n4. **Real-world robustness**: works in noisy environments and without a wake word, which matters on factory floors, shops and reception desks.\n5. **Part of a stack**: AGIBOT positions it as the human-robot interaction foundation model next to **GO-3 Preview** (embodied foundation model) and **Link-U OS** (robot operating system)."
usage: "## Where it fits\n\n- **Reception and retail humanoids** that greet, answer questions and guide visitors in many languages.\n- **Factory and warehouse robots** that need to take spoken instructions over background noise.\n- **Developers on AGIBOT hardware** who want natural conversation without building their own speech pipeline.\n\n## Next steps\n\nWatch AGIBOT's official channels for SDK or API availability and which robot models ship with WITA-Omni 1.0."
