The official account [@AntLingAGI](https://x.com/AntLingAGI/status/2124891223597240338) posted Introducing Ling-3.2-air at 15:42 UTC and pointed to weights on [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/inclusionAI/Ling-3.2-air) and [ModelScope](https://modelscope.cn/models/inclusionAI/Ling-3.2-air).\n\nIt is a sibling of the already-listed **Ling-3.2-flash**, not a rename of that API model. The company says air shares the native multimodal architecture of **Ling-3.0-flash-VL**, with **124B** total parameters and **5.5B** active per token, and is stronger than its predecessor on workflow automation and professional document reasoning. The Hugging Face repo was created earlier the same day at 03:55 UTC; the model card, weights update, and official introduction landed in this scan window. The weights are released under the **MIT** license.\n"
explain: "## Specs and claims\n\nThe model card describes a hybrid-linear MoE: 35 KDA layers plus 7 gated MLA layers (5:1), two dense layers, 512 routed experts and one shared expert, with 8 experts activated per token. Hidden size is 2560 and the vocabulary is 157,184. The model accepts image and video input.\n\nOfficially reported benchmark moves versus the predecessor include:\n\n- **AutomationBench-AA**: 15.7% → **61%**\n- **GDP.pdf** all-pass rate: 8.4% → **16%**\n- **AA-Briefcase v1.1** Elo: 987 → **1340** (+353)\n- **Terminal-Bench 4.0** pass rate: 0% → **27%**\n- **Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5.0.1** score **23**, which the company compares with **GLM-5.3 (Low)** and **DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 (Max)** (about 40B and 49B active, respectively)\n\nOn **Harvey LAB-AA v1.1**, it records an **87.7%** criterion pass rate, but the all-pass rate falls from 8.3% before a material-hallucination check to **0.8%** after it. Those figures are vendor or benchmark-host numbers, not a production SLA.\n\n## Serving\n\nAnt Ling thanked **vLLM** and **SGLang** for day-0 support. The card’s suggested SGLang recipe uses a 256K context on four 141GB-class GPUs (H20-3e / H200) or four-GPU Blackwell nodes, with NEXTN speculative decoding; 80GB cards are advised to use tp=8. Community notes also mention a path up to about 1M context; follow the image and card in front of you. Recommended sampling is temperature=1.0, top_p=0.95, top_k=20, with enable_thinking set to true.\n"
