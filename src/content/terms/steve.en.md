intro: "**Steve** is an autonomous business harness announced on 9 November 2026 by the San Francisco lab [Skyfall AI](https://skyfall.ai/steve), which also markets it as an \"AI CEO.\" In the official post [Introducing Steve](https://skyfall.ai/blog/introducing-steve), the company says it has acquired three software businesses and handed operations to Steve: AlphaSocial (a social media data API), GammaPromo (a Shopify referral app), and DeltaLeads (a Reddit lead-generation tool). Steve took over AlphaSocial and GammaPromo in August and DeltaLeads in October. A human owner still sets goals, permissions, and approval boundaries; production releases, spending above agreed limits, and other gated actions require the owner's approval.\n\nThe architecture described in the blog is a CEO agent that plans with a **world model** of the business as code, then breaks work into tasks for specialist agents in growth, product, engineering, and support. A policy engine is said to classify each action as run automatically, run and notify, request approval, or block. The platform, not the agent, holds short-lived credentials, records evidence (file hashes, exit codes, integration results), and grades whether a task met its criteria. Each company gets its own isolated model. Skyfall also describes historical replays and a recursive self-improvement loop in which proposed lessons and harness updates are judged and, if accepted, tried on sealed past days first.\n\nThe same post publishes early, company-reported outcomes as of 4 November 2026: AlphaSocial monthly revenue more than doubled versus acquisition (paying subscribers 114 → 155), after 54 initiatives and 946 tasks, including an 80% drop in OAuth errors per 1,000 calls and a credit-pack promotion Steve later rolled back because pack sales displaced subscriptions; GammaPromo monthly revenue was down $452, after Steve cut ads that had produced clicks but no paying customers; DeltaLeads, only weeks in, was roughly flat. Live dashboards are linked from the blog. Alongside the launch, Skyfall announced a $2 million Startup Program to acquire five more early-stage companies to run on Steve. The lab is led by Sam Pasupalak, Kaheer Suleman, and Sumit Pasupalak, the team behind Maluuba.\n\nHighlights:\n\n- Product page: https://skyfall.ai/steve ; official blog (9 November 2026): https://skyfall.ai/blog/introducing-steve ;\n- Company site: https://skyfall.ai/ ;\n- The post refers to a versioned harness in press coverage as Steve-001; the official name used on the blog and product page is Steve;\n- This is a bounded experiment: humans still approve consequential actions, and the revenue figures are Skyfall's own operating record, not an independent audit."
