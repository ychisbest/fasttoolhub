Rather than a hand-built physics simulator, it uses a learned world model to create **interactive, repeatable virtual environments** where robot manipulation policies can be validated and improved through trial and error.\n\nAt launch it ranked **#1 on the LIBERO overall leaderboard of WorldSimProbe**, a benchmark for robot world models, winning three of five tests."
