intro: "**HumanWAM** (Human World–Action Model) is the **embodied world–action model family** from **DAGroup-PKU** and collaborators in the **Open-HumanScale** project: pretrain on large-scale egocentric human manipulation video, then adapt to bimanual robots via post-training. Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06817 (arXiv 2611.06817); HF example: https://huggingface.co/DAGroup-PKU/HumanWAM-Vanilla-Base ; project: https://dagroup-pku.github.io/open-humanscale/ .\n\nData & lines:\n\n- **Open-HumanScale-50K**: ~**50,000 hours** of curated egocentric manipulation video with hand-motion and language annotations;\n- **HumanWAM-Vanilla**: pretrained exclusively on human data; the HF Vanilla-Base release includes a human egocentric pretraining checkpoint and an **EgoPro** multi-view mid-training checkpoint for robot post-training init;\n- **HumanWAM-Goal**: joint human–robot pretraining with visual-goal conditioning for zero-shot execution and embodied in-context learning.\n\nPaper-reported post-training results: Vanilla reaches **92.08%** Clean2Clean and **68.88%** Clean2Random on RoboTwin 2.0; Goal reaches **92.70%** / **84.60%** (average **88.65%**). On four real-robot tasks under visual OOD, mean success rises from **2.5%** without egocentric pretraining to **67.5%** with Vanilla."
explain: "## Problem\n\nRobot foundation models need diverse manipulation experience, but teleop data is costly. Egocentric human video scales manipulation priors; Open-HumanScale open-sources the corpus, curation/alignment recipe, and an empirical log-linear scaling law between pretraining hours and downstream success.\n\n## Model lines\n\n- **Vanilla**: human-only world–action pretraining (joint future video + actions), then mid-train / post-train for multi-view robot policies;\n- **Goal**: hybrid human–robot data plus goal-image conditioning for zero-shot control and parameter-free visual in-context adaptation.\n\n## Release status & limits\n\n- HF Vanilla-Base is **weights-first**: pretrained/mid-trained weights are up; matching normalization stats, configs, and runtime resources land separately—downloads alone are **not** a full runnable package;\n- Task robot inference needs the matching post-trained policy and runtime assets;\n- Benchmark numbers are from the paper; reproduce with the official eval suites."
