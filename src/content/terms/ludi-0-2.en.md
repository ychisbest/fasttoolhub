intro: "**Ludi 0.2** is the second version of the robotic intelligence system from **Ludo Robotics**, a Palo Alto-based subsidiary of **KRAFTON** — the Korean game company behind *PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS*. It was presented on **October 30, 2026** at the Bay Area Robotics Symposium (BARS) at UC Berkeley.\n\nLudi is built for **socially intelligent robots**: rather than only executing fixed commands, it aims to understand what a person actually wants, keep track of the conversation, read gestures and adapt as the task changes."
explain: "## Architecture (from Ludi 0.1 paper and launch materials)\n\n- **Reasoning core**: a fine-tuned **vision-language model** (Ludi 0.1 used a Qwen3.5 VLM) trained on multi-turn human–robot interaction traces — ambiguous requests, clarifications, corrections, interruptions.\n- **Harness**: a purpose-built agent loop that decides which \"tool\" to call at each step: speak, navigate, or manipulate.\n- **Skills**: specialized navigation and learned manipulation policies; the demo runs on a **Unitree G1** humanoid with a Dex-3 hand and fine-tuned NVIDIA GR00T manipulation policies.\n\n## What is new in 0.2\n\n- **Social intelligence** – better interpretation of intent, feedback and social cues.\n- **Memory & reasoning** – keeps context through changing tasks.\n- **Tool coordination** – smoother combination of navigation, manipulation and dialogue.\n- **Gesture interpretation** – recognizes and responds to human gestures.\n- **Physical movement** – more adaptable motion.\n\n## Roadmap\n\nLudo says it is working toward **Ludi 1.0**, a unified foundation model integrating perception, dialogue and physical action. Critics noted the 0.2 announcement came without public benchmarks."
