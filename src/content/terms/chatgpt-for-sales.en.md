intro: "**ChatGPT for Sales** is the sales experience [OpenAI introduced on 17 November 2026](https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-for-sales/). The company presents it as a way for sales teams to understand customers better, spend less time on busywork, and close more deals. It combines a new first-party **Sales plugin** with dedicated seller skills, and more than 30 new or expanded partner plugins in ChatGPT, so teams can bring customer context from the tools they already use into one conversation.\n\nThe announcement argues that answers about which accounts to pursue, what a buyer still needs, and which deals need attention usually sit across teammates, prospecting tools, customer calls, and the CRM. ChatGPT for Sales is meant to gather that context for call prep, tailored proposals, and faster follow-up. It is a product experience on top of ChatGPT, not a new foundation-model name; this entry records the product name itself.\n\nA companion page, [Clay for Reps](https://openai.com/business/plugins/clay-for-reps/), is a partner-plugin entry point and is not filed as its own term. The post also describes delegating between conversations to a **dot**, an always-on agent in ChatGPT that can use the same plugins and customer context to prepare meeting briefs, track deal updates, and surface proposed next steps for human review."
explain: "OpenAI groups the workflows around the existing sales stack: learn process and product updates with Confluence, Guru, and Seismic, and rehearse pitches or objections with ChatGPT Voice; build presentations, interactive demos, or ROI calculators with ChatGPT Slides, Gamma, Figma, Canva, or ChatGPT Sites; pull account context from Salesforce, HubSpot, Close CRM, or monday CRM to prepare calls and plan next steps; combine Apollo, Clay, and Common Room with ChatGPT web research to find a reason to reach out, then plan outreach in Outreach or Salesloft; turn conversations into action with the Meetings plugin in ChatGPT, plus Gong, Otter.ai, or Granola; watch pipeline risk with Backstory, the Data plugin, or Hex; and help prepare quotes and agreements with Docusign, Ironclad, PandaDoc, or ServiceNow CPQ when a buyer is ready to move.\n\nCustomer quotes in the post come from the Michael J. Fox Foundation, NeoBid, Pallet, Datasite, and Sculpted Agency, covering prospect research, CRM enrichment, pipeline-risk visibility, and shipping a Clay play in minutes instead of hours or days. These are official testimonials, not independent measurements by this radar. Partner-plugin and feature availability varies by plan, region, and workspace settings."
usage: "According to the official getting-started steps, install the [Sales plugin](https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-for-sales/) first, then add the partner plugins that match your process. ChatGPT Business and Enterprise admins can open the Admin Console, select the workspace, go to Plugins, and either make the Sales plugin and relevant partner plugins available for members or install them by default. Review app access and allowed actions; workspace members complete any required account connections when prompted.\n\nOfficial use cases include building a prioritized account list from CRM data and research tools such as ZoomInfo and Clay; assembling a pre-meeting brief from account history, prior calls, and buyer priorities, then turning it into a customer-specific pitch in Gamma or Canva; drafting the follow-up after a call, capturing agreed next steps, and writing updates back to a connected CRM such as Attio; and asking ChatGPT—or a dot, including via Rox—to flag deals that need attention, always leaving the final decision with the seller. Pricing, permissions, and regional availability are those shown on OpenAI’s pages at the time of use."
