One click.” When an assistant gives a bad answer, hits a rate limit, or you just want a second opinion, it carries the **conversation context** into another AI so you do not have to re-explain.\n\nBy default the text lands in the destination composer for review — **auto-send is off**. You can also copy the full thread as plain text or Markdown into a doc, email, editor, or Slack. The starter plan is 20 free hops a month with no signup.\n\nHighlights:\n\n- Built for switching models mid-thought, not for replacing the chat UI;\n- Reads a thread only when you hop or copy; conversation text is not sent to ChatHop billing and is not used for ad profiles;\n- Works with the major web AI chat services;\n- Site: https://chathop.tech ."
