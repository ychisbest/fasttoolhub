intro: "**Sarvam Extract** is a document-extraction API that **Sarvam AI** released on 24 November 2026: you send a document plus a field schema (or a saved config_id) and get the requested fields back as structured JSON, CSV, or XLSX. The official account [@SarvamAI](https://x.com/SarvamAI/status/2125258873699442823) announced it at 16:02 UTC on 24 November (00:02 on 25 November, Beijing time), with the product post [sarvam.ai/blogs/extract-api](https://www.sarvam.ai/blogs/extract-api). The same blog URL also appeared on the company's sitemap in this scan window.\n\nThe API is powered by **Sarvam Vision 2.1**, a vision model Sarvam published in September 2026—it is not a new base-model name. The company positions Extract as schema-based field extraction on an accuracy-cost trade-off, rather than sending an entire document to a general-purpose model in one shot."
explain: "## What the company claims\n\nFrom the official blog (vendor-reported results on the public ExtractBench benchmark):\n\n- **Two modes**: **Basic** locates each field on a page and reads it there; **Advanced** adds a second pass over the full document and rechecks values where the two readings disagree. Both modes accept documents of up to **500 pages** and return a source page, bounding box, and confidence score for each value.\n- **Accuracy and price**: Advanced scores **95.4 / 100** on ExtractBench at **INR 2.50 per page** (about USD 25 per 1,000 pages); Basic scores **94.4** at **INR 1 per page** (about USD 10 per 1,000 pages). Sarvam says both sit on the accuracy-cost Pareto frontier among the systems it evaluated, including LlamaExtract, Reducto Deep Extract, Pulse, Codex (GPT-6.1 Sol / GPT-6 Luna), and Gemini 3.8 Flash.\n- **Long documents**: on test documents longer than 50 pages, Extract scores **93 / 100**, versus **28** when the whole document is sent to Gemini 3.8 Flash in a single call. A page-first design finds the relevant page for each field before extraction, cutting the context the model has to read.\n- **Tables and lists**: on benchmark documents with lists of 100 or more rows, the company says Advanced returns about **98%** of all rows, versus about **10%** when the whole document goes to Gemini 3.8 Flash in one call.\n- **Source grounding**: when Extract correctly reads a single-value field, the company says it also cites the correct page **98%** of the time.\n- **Languages and deployment**: English and **22 Indian languages**, with values kept in their original scripts. It is offered to users in India and the US, with on-premises deployment for enterprises. Only successfully processed pages are billed.\n\nThe docs describe the [Extract Fields](https://docs.sarvam.ai/api/api-guides-tutorials/document-intelligence/how-to/extract) workflow. The endpoint is POST /doc-ai/v1/job/extract; you must send either schema or config_id, and you can set language, output_format (json, csv, or xlsx), and mode (basic or advanced).\n\n## Relation to earlier Sarvam listings\n\nThis site already lists **Sarvam Prism**, **Sarvam Token Factory**, and **Sarvam Dictate**. Extract is a document-intelligence product API. The model behind it, **Sarvam Vision 2.1**, was introduced in an official blog post dated 24 September 2026, so it is not filed as a new name in this scan."
