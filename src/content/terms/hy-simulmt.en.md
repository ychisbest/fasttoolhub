It performs **bidirectional Chinese↔English speech-to-speech translation**: it continuously ingests audio and translates while the speaker is still talking, producing both translated text and speech, and uses **real-time voice cloning** to keep the speaker's vocal characteristics.\n\nThe core model, **Hy-SimulMT-30B-A3B** (a ~30B-parameter MoE with ~3B active), is on Hugging Face and ModelScope, with the model card listing Apache-2.0. The GitHub repo `Tencent-Hunyuan/Hy-SimulMT` ships the SimulInfer inference service and SimulTrain training tools, and you can also try it in the **Hy Translate app**.\n\n@HuggingPapers summed it up on X: \"Tencent just released Hy-SimulMT on Hugging Face — A real-time simultaneous translation model with ~3s latency and voice cloning.\""
explain: "## Key features\n\n- **Translate while listening**: built for meetings, speeches and interviews, balancing waiting for context against speaking early\n- **Low latency**: on the RealSI long-form benchmark its StreamLAAL (streaming lag) is about **3 seconds**, the lowest of the systems compared (StreamLAAL is not the same as time to first speech)\n- **Quality**: its GEMBA score beats the compared open-source models and four of five commercial APIs\n- **Voice cloning**: target-language speech keeps each speaker's voice, which makes multi-speaker conversations easier to follow (speech output needs a configured TTS service)\n\n## Short-form benchmarks (self-reported, higher is better)\n\n| Model / API | FLEURS | CoVoST2 | ACL6060 | RealSI-Sentence |\n|---|---|---|---|---|\n| **Hy-SimulMT** | 90.3 | 75.5 | 81.2 | **84.7** |\n| Qwen3.8-LiveTranslate | 88.4 | 82.1 | 80.5 | 84.4 |\n| Gemini3.5-LiveTranslate | 92.4 | 74.4 | 82.6 | 82.2 |\n| Seed-LiveInterpret2.0 | 85.8 | 67.7 | 74.8 | 80.0 |\n| GPT-Realtime-Translate | 77.7 | 50.8 | 76.4 | 76.7 |\n\nThe team also ran a small study on 18 new Chinese and English recordings from September 2026 onward (about 6 minutes each, 10 domains) against six interpreters holding CATTI Level 1 or Level 2 certificates. It reports quality on par with expert human interpreters and often lower latency, while stressing that the findings apply only to that sample and setup.\n\n## Model variants\n\n| Model | Description |\n|---|---|\n| Hy-SimulMT-30B-A3B | translation-only output |\n| Hy-SimulMT-30B-A3B-Interleave | source text plus translation |\n| -FP8 / -GGUF versions of both | FP8 quantization / GGUF format |"
usage: "## Deploy with vLLM\n\nYou need Linux, Python 3.12+, Git and an NVIDIA GPU with enough memory.
