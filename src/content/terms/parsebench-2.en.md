The company says it defined 330+ error types, classified as critical, major, minor, or harmless, and used them to calibrate metrics across text, tables, formatting, charts, layout, and forms. The post contrasts a 459-cell table: under ParseBench-1, a swapped number cost about 0.13 points while a correct table returned as Markdown could score zero; under ParseBench-2, the wrong number costs 10 points and the Markdown output deducts 0.
