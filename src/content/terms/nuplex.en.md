Nuplex puts perception and generation in one full-duplex model so a face can react—and start speaking—while you are still talking. Emma turns that claim into a public demo rather than only a launch video, which is the practical way to judge whether a full-duplex audiovisual model actually feels usable.\n\nThe company context matters too: the founders came from Apple work on perception and reconstruction, and in September 2026 they raised a $50 million Series A led by Lightspeed with NVIDIA among the participants.
