Its pitch: learn walking and manipulation together (loco-manipulation) from **first-person human data only**, with no robot present during data collection.\n\nThe paper is arXiv 2610.18950 and the code is on GitHub under Apache 2.0; Pandaily covered the open-source release on October 24, 2026. explain: "## The idea\n\nHumanoid training usually relies on costly, slow real-robot teleoperation, and most egocentric VLA models focus on hands and arms while ignoring torso, posture and locomotion. Lambda-0 uses **robot-free whole-body capture**: collectors wear a neck-mounted GoPro and PICO body trackers and simply do tasks in everyday spaces.\n\n## HumanVerse-500 dataset\n\n- **500 hours** of open-world human loco-manipulation: 32,993 episodes, 87M pose frames, 827 task types\n- Egocentric RGB + 24 tracked body joints + 21 joints per hand (reconstructed from video), all synchronized\n- ~29 s per demo vs ~100 s on a real robot, about **3.4×** collection throughput\n\n## Three-stage training\n\n1. **Egocentric hand-motion pretraining** on seven human datasets\n2. **Robot-aligned whole-body human mid-training**: human demos are converted into the robot's observation/action space (viewpoint matching, rendered G1 arms, coarse-to-fine action alignment)\n3. **Robot post-training** with task-aligned human demos plus native robot demos\n\n## Results (from the paper, not yet independently reproduced)\n\n- On a Unitree G1 (BrainCo Revo2 hands, SONIC whole-body controller): **62.5%** real-world success (25/40), +22.5 pts over the strongest baseline π₀.₅ (40%); 80.4% task progress\n- **90.0%** success on the SIMPLE simulation benchmark\n- More human whole-body data keeps improving real-robot performance"
