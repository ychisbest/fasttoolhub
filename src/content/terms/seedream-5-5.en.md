intro: "**Seedream 5.5** is ByteDance Seed's **next-generation image model**, announced on 9 November 2026. It is aimed at teams that already use text-to-image inside real production — where a result has to stay usable across quality bars, deadlines and budget, not just look good as a one-off sample.\n\nThe official write-up highlights three upgrades:\n\n- **More stable multi-turn editing** — unedited regions stay intact at the pixel level, with less color drift and less detail loss across successive tweaks, supporting continued work on the same image for up to about **100** rounds;\n- **Native RGBA assets** — images can ship with a full or partial alpha channel and cleaner edges, so generated elements drop into a layout without a separate cutout pass;\n- **More realistic materials and clearer text** — lighting, surfaces and skin are meant to look less glossy or blown-out, and complex or densely set small type should hold up better.\n\nProject page: https://seed.bytedance.com/en/seedream5_5 . The matching tech blog went out the same day in English and Chinese. You can try it in Dreamina (https://dreamina.capcut.com/seedream/seedream-5-5 ), CapCut, Doubao, Doubao Workplace, and Volcano Engine Ark / BytePlus as Doubao-Seedream-5.5. A Pro variant of the 5.5 series is promised later."
explain: "## Why it matters\n\nOnce image models enter ecommerce, posters, film and brand work, the failure mode is rarely \"the first frame is ugly.\" It is the tenth edit quietly shifting a color you did not touch, small type falling apart, or a generated object that still needs to be masked before design tools will accept it. Seedream 5.5 treats multi-turn stability and native transparency as first-class features, and ByteDance reports gains over the previous version plus leading scores versus other major models on text structure and text-to-image realism. Chinese coverage also said the API keeps an input-image plus output-image billing split (text tokens free) and put a 2K square edit at roughly ¥0.28 — less than half of Seedream 5.0 Pro — subject to the live Ark price list.\n\n## Limits\n\nDay-one distribution is mostly inside ByteDance surfaces (Dreamina, Doubao, CapCut, Volcano Engine Ark / BytePlus); third-party access is not fully spelled out. The Pro model is not out yet."
