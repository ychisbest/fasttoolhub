intro: "**GigaChat 4** is the new language-model family that the Russian bank Sber officially announced on 26 November 2026 at [AI Journey 2026](https://sberbusiness.live/news/ai-journey-2026-kakie-novinki-prezentoval-sber) in Moscow. The canonical write-up is the [GigaChat blog post](https://giga.chat/blog/posts/gigachat-4); the [English page](https://giga.chat/blog/en/posts/gigachat-4) was still being translated at the time of this note and points to a [technical report](https://giga.chat/blog/en/posts/gigachat-4-tech-report).\n\nSber says the release includes two models: a **GigaChat 4 708B** flagship with 708 billion total parameters and about 41 billion active ([ai-sage/GigaChat4-708B-A41B](https://huggingface.co/ai-sage/GigaChat4-708B-A41B) on Hugging Face), and a compact **GigaChat 4 37B** trained from scratch rather than distilled from the flagship ([ai-sage/GigaChat4-37B-A4B](https://huggingface.co/ai-sage/GigaChat4-37B-A4B)). Both are described as having a native context of about one million tokens, and both offer instant (answer immediately) and reasoning (think first) modes in a single checkpoint. The 708B model card lists an MIT license. Code and the technical report live at [salute-developers/gigachat4](https://github.com/salute-developers/gigachat4).\n\nIn the previous radar window (20:36–00:36 Beijing time on 26 November) the name appeared only once, in a zero-like unofficial roundup. The official blog is dated 26 November, and the 708B model card was updated at 17:48 UTC (01:48 on 27 November, Beijing time). This window treats that as a confirmed official launch. Capabilities and benchmark comparisons below are Sber’s own account.\n"
explain: "## What the company claims\n\nThe account below comes from the 26 November [official blog post](https://giga.chat/blog/posts/gigachat-4) and the [technical report page](https://giga.chat/blog/en/posts/gigachat-4-tech-report). It is the vendor’s description.\n\nSber presents GigaChat 4 as a model family that can not only answer questions but also work for hours in agent environments: it plans, calls tools, checks results and corrects mistakes. The post says both sizes can run inside harnesses such as Claude Code, Codex, pi, DeepSeek’s agent harness and OpenHands, including repository work, file edits, tool calls and output checks.\n\n### Scale and official comparisons\n\nThe technical-report abstract describes the 708B model as a hybrid mixture-of-experts system with 708B total parameters, 41B active, and a native context length of one million tokens. Sber’s own evaluations put the 37B next to Qwen3.6-35B-A3B, and the 708B next to GLM 5.3, DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017 and Mistral-Large-4-1T-A52B. Those comparisons are the vendor’s, not independent replications.\n\n### Architecture and decoding\n\nSber also says both models were trained on sequences up to about one million tokens during context extension, and that it trained a diffusion drafter, DFlash2, from scratch, claiming a higher average number of accepted tokens than its previous best end-to-end MTP on math and code. Training of the 37B used μP to transfer hyperparameters from a ten-times-smaller model, with Muon to improve training efficiency. Finer 708B configurations are in the GitHub report; a separate 37B report is described as forthcoming.\n\n### Official demos\n\nThe post’s 708B experiments include flipping the palette in a NES Mario cartridge and running it on a Mac M4, generating a 3D-printable vase STL from a text prompt, and writing a multi-track piece plus a visualisation page through an MCP connection to the Cadenza music workstation. These are official demos, not a guarantee they will reproduce in every setup.\n\n## Limits\n\nSber operates under export controls and compute constraints, and independent public review of training data, safety alignment and third-party benchmarks is still limited. Official scores are in-house. Check the licence on each card before use (the 708B card states MIT; the 37B card has been tagged license:other—defer to the live page) and test on your own tasks. This is a weights-and-report release, not a claim that the models are the default on major global consumer chat apps.\n"
