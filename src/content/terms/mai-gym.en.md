intro: "**MAI-Gym** is an **open, all-in-one platform for GUI-agent evaluation, data collection and online reinforcement learning (RL)** across **mobile, computer and web** environments, released by the **MAI-UI team at Alibaba (Tongyi / Alibaba Token Hub)**. The paper *\"MAI-Gym: An Open All-in-One Platform for GUI Agent Evaluation and RL Training across Mobile and Computer Environments\"* (arXiv:2610.20939) appeared in mid-October 2026 and was picked up by Chinese AI accounts on X on **October 31, 2026**.\n\nAlongside the framework, the team trained and released **MAI-Gym-9B** (from Qwen3.5-9B), a single agent for both phone and desktop use that reaches **78.4% on MobileWorld** and **76.2% on OSWorld-Verified** — state of the art among open GUI agents under 10B parameters. MAI-Gym was also used to train Alibaba's Qwen-UI-Agent."
explain: "## Key facts\n\n- **Developer:** MAI-UI team, Alibaba Group (Tongyi Lab / Alibaba Token Hub); corresponding authors include Steven Hoi\n- **Paper:** arXiv:2610.20939 (submitted Oct 14, 2026)\n- **Scope:** one shared rollout pipeline for evaluation **and** training, so agents behave identically in both\n- **Benchmarks:** 10 established benchmarks supported (MobileWorld, AndroidWorld, OSWorld-Verified, OSWorld 2.0, MacAgentBench, MyPCBench, MobileGym, …)\n- **Scale:** up to **20,000 parallel environment instances**\n- **Synthesis:** automatically generates GUI apps and tasks — **49 resettable mocked apps**, coherent cross-app worlds, execution-validated task verifiers\n- **Released:** framework, synthesis skills, mocked apps, **2,000 verified tasks**, and the **MAI-Gym-9B** checkpoint\n\n## MAI-Gym-9B training recipe\n\n1. **SFT warm-up** on mixed mobile + desktop GUI/CLI data.\n2. **Joint RL** (GRPO) on 5,000 synthesized tasks from MobileWorld and OSWorld environments in a single run — instead of training per-domain experts and merging weights.\n3. Code-verified rewards, policy-adaptive task sampling and windowed history; ~96 hours on 512 GPUs.\n\n## Results\n\n- 78.4% MobileWorld, 76.2% OSWorld-Verified (SOTA among open GUI agents < 10B)\n- RL in a **mocked train-ticketing app transferred to the real app**, raising success on a physical phone from 28.8% to 48.1%\n- Training on entirely novel synthesized apps improved held-out success from 25.8% to 45.2%\n\n## Why it matters\n\nGUI-agent research has suffered from fragmented evaluation harnesses and expensive interactive environments. MAI-Gym offers a reproducible, open stack that unifies them — useful for anyone building phone-use or computer-use agents."
