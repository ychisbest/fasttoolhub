intro: "**WALL-OSS-1.0** is a vision-language-action (**VLA**) foundation model from Shenzhen-based embodied-AI company **X Square Robot** (自变量机器人), announced under the line “Understand More. Act Better.” The official account [@XSquareRobot](https://x.com/XSquareRobot/status/2124767551959757162) posted the introduction at 07:30 UTC on **23 November 2026** (15:30 Beijing time). A same-day GlobeNewswire release (02:44 ET) gives the fuller technical and evaluation write-up. Product page: [x2robot.com/en](https://x2robot.com/en). Press release: [GlobeNewswire, 23 November 2026](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/23/3408046/0/en/x-square-robot-introduces-wall-oss-1-0-understand-more-act-better-a-native-foundation-model-for-the-physical-world.html).\n\nThe company says the model uses **native VLA pretraining**: instead of training a vision-language model first and adding robot actions later, it starts from a **Qwen3.5** base and jointly trains on language, vision-language, and robot-action data. The release also describes an improved action tokenizer, a **flow-matching Action Expert** for continuous control, and real-time chunking for smoother successive movements. The family includes **Lite, Nano, Gen, and AR** variants. Its predecessor is the already-published **WALL-OSS-0.5**.\n\nThe same launch introduces two companions: **XR Harness**, an agentic framework (developer preview) that connects high-level planning with physical execution, and **WALL-API-1.0**, a developer-facing interface. A follow-up official post at 08:07 UTC said WALL-API-1.0 is now available for calling WALL-OSS-1.0. The real-robot and simulation figures in the release are **vendor-reported**. As of the announcement, the main public Hugging Face artifacts were earlier **WALL-OSS-1.0-Lite RoboDojo** preview checkpoints, which are not the full 1.0 weights behind the 312-task real-robot evaluation. Parameter counts and API pricing had not been published.\n"
explain: "## What problem it targets\n\nThe release argues that language and vision models have advanced faster than the ability to turn that understanding into reliable physical action. Many existing VLA systems pretrain a vision-language model and only later add robot actions. WALL-OSS-1.0 is positioned as a native-pretraining alternative, so vision, language, and action shape the model together from the start, with the aim of transferring to unfamiliar tasks and environments rather than fitting a separate policy to each new setting.\n\n## Technical points from the official release\n\n- **Training path**: native VLA pretraining on a Qwen3.5 base; an improved action tokenizer turns continuous robot motion into compact discrete tokens; objectives include not only imitating demonstrations but also understanding actions, evaluating candidate behaviors, and correcting errors.\n- **Action generation**: a flow-matching Action Expert maps visual and language context into continuous robot commands, with real-time chunking to keep successive movements smooth.\n- **Instruction layers**: trajectories are annotated at four levels—task, subtask, action, and segment—so the model can follow both broad goals and tight constraints on objects, poses, and motion. That is the stated basis for **XR Harness** directing manipulation skills in natural language.\n- **Companion systems**: **XR Harness** has three layers—System 2 interprets goals, plans, and revises from feedback; System 1 coordinates execution (WALL-OSS-1.0 generates actions and checks completion); System 0 talks to controllers and sensors through the Robot Agent Protocol (RAP). **WALL-API-1.0** exposes the model through an API.\n\n## Vendor-reported results (treat as company figures)\n\n- Open-ended real robots: evaluators freely chose 312 unseen tasks (unfamiliar objects, tool use, bimanual coordination, multi-step work, and so on). The release reports a **55.1%** zero-shot success rate with no task-specific demonstrations or fine-tuning.\n- Simulation **X2Real** (40-plus reasoning and manipulation tasks, models fine-tuned on the same simulation data): WALL-OSS-1.0 scored **67.55 / 100**, 65.0% above the release’s strongest open-source baseline, **Lingbot-VLA-2.0**, at 40.94. On out-of-distribution mobile manipulation the mean was 44.17 versus 24.83 for π0.5.\n- Public **RoboDojo** simulation leaderboard: as of 5 November 2026, compact **WALL-OSS-1.0-Lite** ranked first among policies without agent systems, with an overall score of 42.59.\n- Real-world **ManipArena** (15 tabletop tasks): **1,101 / 1,500** after pretraining and without task-specific fine-tuning, which the release says is 52.5% above predecessor WALL-OSS-0.5 and 70.8% above a multi-task fine-tuned π0.5 baseline. It led or tied 14 of 15 tasks. Overall success was 50.67%, versus 19.33% for 0.5 and 24.0% for π0.5.\n\nThese numbers come from the company release and have not been independently replicated here. They should not be treated as guaranteed performance in arbitrary settings. Full 1.0-family weights, parameter counts, and pricing were still incomplete at announcement.\n"
