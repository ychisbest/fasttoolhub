intro: "**FrontierPhysics** is an **end-to-end physics research** agent benchmark. Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06910 (arXiv:2611.06910).\n\nActive physicists (PhD students, postdocs, research scientists) author tasks from **their own research**: **56** tasks, **42** physicists, **26** institutions, across theoretical, computational, and experimental physics in **nine** primary domains. Given a PI-style high-level prompt, agents must review literature, plan, run research/experiments, and produce deterministically verifiable numerical outputs plus a scientific manuscript (e.g. paper.pdf).\n\nHighlights:\n\n- Combines **deterministic verifiers** (scripts check numerical outputs) with **expert-authored rubrics** (blockers / non-blockers);\n- A pass requires the verifier **and** every blocker; non-blockers weight manuscript/research quality;\n- About **22** models in **26** configurations; early results: **no** configuration passes more than **46** of **168** rollouts; **Claude Opus 5.5** overall Pass@1 ≈ **27.4%**;\n- Manual trajectory review: main weaknesses are **insufficiently rigorous reasoning** and **limited expert intuition**, not missing physics facts or pure execution errors."
explain: "## Problem\n\nPrior physics benchmarks mostly use fixed QA, isolated sub-tasks, or paper reproduction with the original paper given. FrontierPhysics asks agents to do open-ended, multi-stage research without a reference solution, grading both **correctness** and **publishable quality**.\n\n## Tasks & scoring\n\n- Tasks come from contributors’ own projects (typically ≥ ~two weeks of real work); some underlie Nature / PRL / PRX papers;\n- Deliverables: deterministic artifacts + manuscript; global rubrics cover reward hacking, hallucination, literature synthesis; task rubrics are expert-written;\n- Score is 0 if the verifier or any blocker fails; otherwise a weighted non-blocker score measures quality.\n\n## Limits\n\n- v0.1 with 56 tasks—per-domain gaps remain uncertain;\n- Agent-as-judge grading can err (authors report and corrected some false negatives);\n- Failure-mode analysis focuses on a strong-model cohort; other models/harnesses may differ."
