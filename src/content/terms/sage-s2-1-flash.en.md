intro: "**Sage-S2.1-Flash** (Chinese official name **书生 S2.1 Flash**) is a scientific multimodal agent model formally released on 16 November 2026 by Shanghai Artificial Intelligence Laboratory’s InternLM / 书生 line. The official page at [internlm.github.io/Sage-S2.1-Flash](https://internlm.github.io/Sage-S2.1-Flash/) opens with “今天，我们正式发布 书生 S2.1 Flash” (“Today, we formally release 书生 S2.1 Flash”). It describes a frontier scientific multimodal agent model for demanding research: a 320-billion-parameter MoE that combines scientific multimodal understanding with general agent capabilities, for scientific deep research, scientific programming, and operation of specialized research software.\n\nThe lab says the model unifies more than ten kinds of scientific input — including molecular graphs, crystal structures, protein sequences and 3D structures, DNA/RNA sequences, spectra, mass spectra, X-ray diffraction, ECG, EEG, and light curves — under an architecture named **Sage SciPerception**. It also says the model was trained in large-scale verifiable environments to strengthen search, programming, tool use, and multi-step execution, and can inspect software state and intermediate results via visual feedback before revising its next steps. Official demos include recovering a single CT series from mixed slices and reconstructing bone in 3D Slicer, 3D modeling in Blender / CAD, nucleus segmentation in Fiji, and interactive literature-backed scientific analysis.\n\nThe official evaluation table covers 30-plus benchmarks. Among them it lists 90.3 on MLE-bench Lite, 72.2 on OfficeQA Pro, 25.0 on Harvey LAB, and 37.1 on SpreadsheetBench 2, first on those four among the comparison set shown (GLM-5.3-Flash, DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017, Qwen3.8-Max, GPT-5.6 Sol, and Claude Opus 5). Those figures are the lab’s own evaluations; this page does not independently reproduce them.\n\nJiqizhixin reported at 19:37 Beijing time the same day that the model was jointly released by Shanghai AI Laboratory with Fudan University, the Institute of Software of the Chinese Academy of Sciences, Renmin University of China, the Institute of Automation of the Chinese Academy of Sciences, East China Normal University, Harbin Institute of Technology, and Peking University, and linked a demo, Hugging Face, ModelScope, and a technical report. The same article also described a limited-time API promotion against GLM 5.3 Flash: 1% of the comparison model’s price through 9 December, then 10% from 10–31 December, plus a new-user credit grant — confirm current terms on the official demo page.
