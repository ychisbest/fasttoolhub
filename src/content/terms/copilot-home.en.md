intro: "**Copilot Home** is the name for **Home**, the starting place in the Microsoft Copilot app that Microsoft declared generally available at Ignite on 17 November 2026. The official Copilot blog [Copilot at Microsoft Ignite…](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/blog/2026/11/17/copilot-at-microsoft-ignite-expanding-whats-possible-with-intelligence-built-for-work/) describes Home as the place where Chat and Cowork come together, now GA. The companion post [Home: Your starting place for work in the Microsoft Copilot app](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoft-copilot-blog/home-your-starting-place-for-work-in-the-microsoft-copilot-app/4573945) defines it as a single entry point for asking questions, delegating multi-step tasks, seeing what needs attention, and tracking what Copilot is creating and running.\n\nIn Home, Auto routes a task to models and effort levels. For more complex, delegated work, Auto may also recommend moving from Chat to Cowork and will carry the prompt and relevant context across. Official materials also say Office capabilities are generally available in Chat and Cowork, so users can create editable Word, Excel, and PowerPoint files saved to OneDrive. A Today view brings mail, calendar, Teams, and other items that need attention into Home.\n\nThis entry records the GA surface name Copilot Home / Home. It is not a new foundation model, and it should not be merged with same-event names such as Microsoft Perception, the MAI models, or Copilot Autopilot, which was described as still rolling out to Frontier customers."
explain: "Microsoft presents Home as the “start work here” layer inside the Copilot app, not a separate chatbot brand. Asking a question (Chat) and handing off a longer piece of work (Cowork) had been easier to treat as different doors; the GA is about putting them in one Home, with shared history and one composer. Auto picks models and effort. On the Cowork side, official copy also says Auto uses Microsoft-Decision-1.1 to adjust effort and can split complex work across sub-agents running in parallel — described at the time as rolling out to Frontier.\n\nThe same event included other Copilot changes: Office in Copilot GA, Copilot Managed Runtime GA, Code coming to GA in the following weeks, and Autopilot beginning to reach Frontier customers. Those are related but distinct names and should not all be stuffed into this Home entry. Today is described as a more proactive command center inside Home, so work does not always have to start from a typed prompt.\n\nWhich tenants already see Home, Auto, or Today follows the Microsoft 365 / Copilot app and release notes at the time you look. Copilot Home is not a replacement for Microsoft 365 as a whole; it is the starting layer inside the Copilot app."
