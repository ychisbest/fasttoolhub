intro: "**FineART** (Fine-Grained Annotated Robotic Trajectory) is a **bimanual robot manipulation dataset** co-released by **Scale AI** and **Hugging Face LeRobot** — Scale AI's **first open-source robotics dataset and model**. Scale Labs dated the release **2026-10-29**, and this week it is climbing the Hugging Face dataset trending list (`lerobot/FineART`, 840k+ downloads).\n\nScale:\n\n- **40,543** teleoperated episodes, **1,718 hours**, 185.5M frames;\n- **533,913** timestamped natural-language subtask annotations (~13 per episode);\n- **151** tasks — ~77% pick-and-place/sorting, 23% contact-rich long tail (textiles, cables, tool use, precision insertion);\n- Collected on a Trossen Stationary AI (ALOHA-style) bimanual robot with 4 cameras; LeRobot dataset v3.0 format; **CC BY 4.0**.\n\nAlongside it ships **FineART-VLA**, an extension of Physical Intelligence's π0.5 combining a System-2 subtask predictor and System-1 continuous-action controller in one policy."
explain: "## What problem it solves\n\nMost open robot datasets provide one task-level instruction per episode (\"unpack the box\") or annotate subtasks for only part of their hours, which limits hierarchical \"plan the next step, then act\" policies. FineART densely annotates **100% of its hours**, claiming to be the **largest subtask-annotated open robot dataset**.\n\n## Highlights\n\n- **Two-level annotation**: task-level plus temporally segmented subtasks, with execution-quality/success metadata;\n- **Three-stage pipeline**: separate teams collect, annotate and validate;\n- **FineART-VLA (self-reported)**: mid-training lifts spatial instruction following from 32% to 100%; with human step-by-step subtask guidance, unseen long-horizon success rises from 16% to 76%; matches baseline on a new robot with ~10× less fine-tuning data.\n\n## Compared with peers\n\nVersus instruction-per-episode datasets like Open X-Embodiment or DROID, FineART is better suited to System-1/System-2 hierarchical VLAs, but covers a single hardware platform (ALOHA-style bimanual)."

Load: `from lerobot.datasets.lerobot_dataset import LeRobotDataset; ds = LeRobotDataset(\"lerobot/FineART\")`;\n3. Use `lerobot-annotate` to segment subtasks in your own LeRobot dataset;\n4. Fine-tune from `lerobot/FineART-VLA-subtask-midtrained` with `lerobot-train`.\n\n## Notes\n\nAt ~1,718 hours of video, a full download needs substantial storage and bandwidth; stream task subsets first."
