intro: "**Businesses in ChatGPT** is a beta product OpenAI announced on 10 November 2026. In the official [introduction](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt/), businesses can use **Business Hub** to claim their organization, keep details current, add self-service product feeds with pricing and availability, and see aggregated insights on how people engage with them in ChatGPT. Eligible U.S. ecommerce and online-service businesses can also build a **Business Agent**: when a business appears in a model response, a customer can open a clearly labeled conversation — separate from ChatGPT's independent answers — to explore offerings. OpenAI posted the announcement as [@OpenAI](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2120216592395174382). There is also a [product page](https://openai.com/business/businesses-in-chatgpt/).\n\nThe post says ChatGPT is used by 1.2 billion people each week. Any eligible business in a region where ChatGPT is available can claim via Business Hub; existing OpenAI Enterprise customers and advertisers can work with their representative; Shopify merchants can connect from Business Hub or the Shopify admin. Insights include mention counts, website clicks, and discovery topics (common themes in conversations that cite the business). OpenAI says insights are aggregated, appear only above certain thresholds, and that user conversations and Business Agent chats are not shared with businesses.\n\nBusinesses can also sponsor their Business Agent and use it as a destination for ChatGPT Ads; sponsored placements are described as clearly labeled and separate from ChatGPT responses. The company frames today's launch as an early step toward bringing businesses more deeply into ChatGPT, and says it plans to expand Businesses in ChatGPT and Business Agents to more markets and categories.\n\nHighlights:\n\n- Official blog (10 November 2026): https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt/ ;\n- Product page: https://openai.com/business/businesses-in-chatgpt/ ;\n- Official announcement: https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2120216592395174382 ;\n- **Business Hub** and **Business Agent** are parts of this product, not unrelated names."
