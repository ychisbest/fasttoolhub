intro: "**Axion 1.0** is an open **agent foundation model family** from **ByteDance**, described in the paper *Axion 1.0: Open Agent Foundation Models, Simplified* (HF Papers 2611.03233, posted 2026-11-02), and widely discussed on **2026-11-05**.\n\nIt comes in two main configurations:\n\n- **Axion 1.0-35B** (35B total / 3B active, initialized from Qwen3.6-35B-A3B)\n- **Axion 1.0-122B** (122B total / 10B active, initialized from Qwen3.5-122B-A10B)\n\nThe paper also reports SFT-only 4B and 9B variants. The headline is *simplicity*: roughly **18K SFT examples**, two SFT stages plus one RL stage, and no dedicated mid-training, DPO or on-policy distillation, yet competitive results on agentic science and search benchmarks such as **HLE, SEAL-0, FrontierScience, BioMysteryBench and HiPhO**. Code and recipe live at GitHub `bytedance/axion1_0`; a community reproduction of the 35B checkpoint is on Hugging Face at `Axion1-0/Axion-1.0-35B` (Apache 2.0)."
explain: "## What problem it solves\n\nBuilding a research-style agent model that can search, browse, run Python and query scholarly sources usually requires heavy pipelines: mid-training, DPO, on-policy distillation. Axion 1.0 argues that **a small amount of verifiable, high-quality data plus a simple three-stage recipe** is enough to be competitive.\n\n## Key numbers (official Table 1)\n\n| Model | HLE | SEAL-0 | FS Olympiad | FS Research |\n|---|---|---|---|---|\n| Axion 1.0-35B | 52.2 | **62.2** | 86.0 | 56.7 |\n| Axion 1.0-122B | **59.0** | 58.6 | **87.0** | **66.7** |\n\n- vs. the similarly sized Agents-A1, the 35B gains +4.6 on HLE and +5.8 on SEAL-0.\n- The 122B scores 66.7 on FrontierScience Research vs. 63.3 for GPT-5.6 Sol (team-run evaluation).\n\n## Recipe\n\n1. Two SFT stages (full-parameter, sample packing).\n2. Asynchronous **GRPO** RL with 8 rollouts per question and session-level training across context-condensation events.\n3. Reasoning problems hardened using base-model feedback; tool tasks built from public data with programmatic reference answers.\n\n## Caveats\n\n- The official repo mainly ships the paper and recipe; the 35B weights on HF are a **community reproduction**, not an official checkpoint.\n- Cross-source baselines use different tools/settings, so comparisons are indicative.\n- It targets scientific reasoning and information seeking, not general chat or coding."
