intro: "**Wanaka Studio** is the web-based **AI-native 3D game engine** of AI game platform **Wanaka**, which released **version 1.0 on November 2, 2026**. You describe a game idea in a sentence or pick a template; AI generates the project structure, characters, animations, textures and worlds; you then fine-tune in a visual 3D editor and publish with one click, sharing a **browser link** friends can play with no code and no download.\n\nWanaka was founded in May 2025 by ex-ByteDance entrepreneur **Zhang Yang** and has raised several million dollars from Jinqiu Fund, Old Friendship Fund, Ubiquant Ventures and others at a reported ~$50M valuation. The full product is pitched as a \"CapCut + TikTok\" for games: the web studio for creation, a mobile app for distribution and social play."
explain: "## Key capabilities\n\n- **Idea to playable project**: a creation agent walks you through building step by step; genres include racers, shooters, platformers, tower defense, puzzles, sims and multiplayer arenas.\n- **All-in-one asset generation**: AI character creator, animation generator (rigged, looping motions), PBR textures and materials, and a World Builder for full environments.\n- **AI game team**: helps plan, generate assets and audio, and even promote your game.\n- **Human/AI split**: AI handles code, assets and animation; humans handle spatial layout, interaction and pacing. The founder says about 70% of creation can be done by AI, while scene building stays hands-on, \"like building with LEGO.\"\n- **Social and remix**: community games can be played and remixed; the mobile app turns photos of real objects into 3D characters for your games.\n\n## Version 1.0 claims\n\n- Up to 5x faster creation than rivals at lower cost;\n- Multiplayer, physics and sound built in;\n- Claimed 4K 60fps visuals with cross-platform browser play.\n\nPerformance figures are company claims and have not been independently verified."
