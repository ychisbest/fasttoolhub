intro: "**PAN 2** is an open foundation world model for physical intelligence, announced on 10 November 2026 by the Institute of Foundation Models (IFM) at MBZUAI. On the official [product page](https://ifm.ai/pan/) and in the announcement from [@IFM_AI](https://x.com/IFM_AI/status/2120199814696828942), the lab's line is: a language model predicts the next word; PAN 2 predicts the next world — what happens after each action, grounded in time, physics and space — so people and AI agents can simulate “what if?” before they act. MBZUAI's account [@mbzuai](https://x.com/mbzuai/status/2120202298119045374) posted congratulations and said the release includes model weights plus training and inference code.\n\nThe official page describes the method as generative latent prediction (GLP), with two reasoning backbones. A multimodal LLM does conceptual reasoning: it works out what happens and when, including the timing of actions and camera motion. A 29-billion-parameter latent diffusion model, trained from scratch, does perceptual reasoning: it fills in geometry, appearance, and how things move. The page illustrates the design with examples such as a surfer's pop-up timing, milk versus ink versus oil poured into water, and a camera that can orbit a scene without rewriting the world it shows.\n\nIFM presents PAN 2 as a rehearsal space for agents and humans before real-world acting, not merely a video generator optimized for looks. A Hugging Face repo is listed at [IFM/PAN2](https://huggingface.co/IFM/PAN2). License, hardware needs, and exact checkpoints should be taken from the official page and the repository card.\n\nHighlights:\n\n- Official page: https://ifm.ai/pan/ ;\n- Official announcement (10 November 2026): https://x.com/IFM_AI/status/2120199814696828942 ;\n- MBZUAI congratulations: https://x.com/mbzuai/status/2120202298119045374 ;\n- Hugging Face: https://huggingface.co/IFM/PAN2 ;\n- This is a new generation name on IFM's world-model line. It is not the same thing as unrelated “PAN” shared-task or project names."

The Hugging Face repository may predate this public announcement; use the 10 November product page and official post as the public launch, and check the current repo docs for APIs, license, and dependencies. Do not confuse PAN 2 with other projects or evaluation tasks that also use the name “PAN.”"
