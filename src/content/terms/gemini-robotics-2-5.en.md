intro: "**Gemini Robotics 2.5** is a vision-language-action (VLA) model announced on 10 November 2026 by Google DeepMind. In a blog post signed by Carolina Parada, the lab calls it its most capable VLA model to date: it is meant to increase both the range of skills a robot can learn and the speed at which it can acquire them, with improvements the post groups under dexterity, reliability, and safety. DeepMind posted the announcement as [@GoogleDeepMind](https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2120169787896525104). There is also a [model page](https://deepmind.google/models/gemini-robotics/vla/).\n\nThe lab says it evaluated the model on more than 200 distinct tasks — examples include serving food with tongs, tying a bow on a gift box, and pipetting a liquid — and reports a 30% to 100% higher success rate depending on the task type, plus 20% to 30% faster completion, versus previous baselines. As with earlier Gemini Robotics models, it is described as working across robot types, including Apptronik's Apollo 2 humanoid (with the five-finger, 22-DoF SharpaWave hand) and a Franka Duo platform with two-finger parallel grippers.\n\nFor a real-world check, DeepMind partnered with Apptronik and Mercedes-Benz on a proof of concept at production lines in Kecskemét, Hungary, and Sindelfingen, Germany. In the lab's account, Apollo 2 used an embodied reasoning model (Gemini Robotics ER) to track the workflow and Gemini Robotics 2.5 to control hands and arms, picking metal brackets from supply bins into sequencing trays, with a 96% success rate. The post says the model is rolling out to DeepMind's robotics partners. Development was accelerated, the lab writes, by **Gemini 4 Argon** acting as a simulation engine (generating varied practice runs and digital twins from camera scenes).\n\nHighlights:\n\n- Official blog (10 November 2026): https://deepmind.google/blog/gemini-robotics-2-5-accelerates-real-world-physical-intelligence/ ;\n- Official announcement: https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2120169787896525104 ;\n- Model page: https://deepmind.google/models/gemini-robotics/vla/ ;\n- Distinct from **Gemini 4 Carbon** and **Gemini 4 Argon**, which are general frontier models; this name is the robotics VLA line. The same post also introduces **ASIMOV-Jailbreak**, an open safety benchmark for robotics jailbreaks (the post cites a 99.5% refusal rate for Argon on that set, versus 82% for a prior Gemini Robotics ER 2 baseline)."
