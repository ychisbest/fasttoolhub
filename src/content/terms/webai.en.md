explain: "## Key points

1. **Pitch**: local inference built into the browser so page content need not leave the device.
2. **APIs**: **Prompt**, **Decision**, and **Semantic Embeddings** for generation, classification, and relatedness.
3. **Models**: open-weight models via Opera's engine plus compatible on-device models (including Google Gemma; Apple's on-device model on Apple silicon).
4. **Control**: opt-in; nothing downloads until enabled; can be turned off anytime.
5. **Platforms**: early preview on 64-bit Windows (Intel/AMD) and Apple-silicon Macs — not Intel Macs, Linux, or Windows on Arm. Speed depends on hardware.
6. ** demos**: six experimental extensions (Project Lens, Got It?, Semantic Art, etc.).

## Why this radar term

Concrete proper noun **WebAI** (not a generic \"browser AI\" phrase) with an official Opera post, press, and same-day PH discussion; not previously in this radar DB."
usage: "## Get started

1. Install/update [Opera One](https://www.opera.com/).
2. Settings → Opera AI → enable **Web AI API** (model download may take a few minutes).
3. Follow [next.opera.com/web-ai](https://next.opera.com/web-ai) or try the sample extensions.

## Tips

- Still an early preview — APIs and model catalog may change.
- Local inference taxes the device; low-end hardware will feel slow.
- \"Web standard\" status is proposed; cross-browser adoption is TBD."
