intro: "**ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview** is the foundation model Baidu released on 11 November 2026: the first post-trained model built on the earlier **ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base**, officially positioned as an open-weight model designed for efficient inference. The English product page is [Introducing ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview](https://ernie.baidu.com/release/ernie-5-5-flash-preview/), and the Chinese page is [ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview 发布](https://ernie.baidu.com/zh/release/ernie-5-5-flash-preview/). The company account [@Baidu_Inc](https://x.com/Baidu_Inc/status/2120391732248637734) announced it at 2026-11-11T05:42:38Z; the model account [@Baidu_ERNIE](https://x.com/Baidu_ERNIE/status/2120425542688379314) repeated the specs and said the weights will be open-sourced by 25 November.\n\nAccording to the official product page, the model has 280 billion total parameters, 14 billion active per token, and a 1-million-token context, with the same limit on input and output. Officially, it is stronger at coding, 3D modeling, knowledge work and content creation, while balancing capability with inference efficiency and keeping decoding fast. @Baidu_Inc also said generation speed is above 200 tokens per second, and that it posts competitive scores on Terminal-Bench, AutomationBench and other benchmarks. Those figures come from official materials, and some comparisons may be Baidu’s own runs; this page does not treat them as independent third-party results. The API is live on Baidu’s Qianfan platform; the company later posted in the same thread that the model can be tried for free on [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/baidu/ernie-5.5-flash-preview:free). Weights are planned for release under Apache 2.0 by 25 November; they were not out at the time of this scan.\n\nOfficial demos ran end-to-end in an agent harness and credit external assets and tools. For games, the page says the model works with Unreal Engine, Unity, Godot and other major engines. In one official case, it merged assets made for several Unreal Engine generations into a UE 5.8 project and wrote a third-person wuxia game, *Yunxiu Xing*, in C++ in a single 11-hour coding task. Because it is a text-only model and cannot see the screen, Baidu says it turned playtest problems — a character hovering, an inconsistent running direction, a misplaced camera — into measurable tests in code, then iterated with a growing test suite and playtest feedback. For 3D and design, demos include building an airport and an airliner with the Blender Python API, reconstructing Hōryū-ji, and writing editable FreeCAD models of an engine, a building and a lunar-rover concept. For knowledge work, official examples include research reports, a personal wealth-planning workbook and an original orchestral piece. For video, the page says the model can take a job from research and script through storyboard to a finished film; shorts such as *The Pelican Test* were drawn and composited in Python. Official cases repeatedly used tools such as Seedance 2.5 and image generators, plus third-party assets, all credited on the page.\n\nThe official page also states the limits: this is an **intermediate checkpoint** from post-training, some capabilities may still fall short, training is ongoing, and the official release will improve based on real-world use and feedback.
