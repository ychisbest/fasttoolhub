intro: "**KineDB** is a database engine designed to run **both on local devices and in backend infrastructure**, with **offline sync built into the database itself**. Founded by Axel Loewenhoff and Allan Zimmermann, the company raised **€850,000 in pre-seed funding** led by Superhero Capital, as reported by Tech.eu and RuntimeWire on **October 30, 2026**. Its tagline: *\"Your servers are optional.\"*"
explain: "## The idea\n\nOffline-capable apps today usually stitch together a **local store** (e.g. SQLite on the device), a **server database**, and a separate **sync layer** to reconcile them. KineDB's bet is that **one engine** can play all three roles, which would simplify the architecture for local-first and mobile apps.\n\n## Why now\n\nThe founders frame it around AI reshaping software development: as more code is generated quickly, simpler infrastructure with fewer moving parts becomes more valuable.\n\n## Caveats\n\nKineDB is at a **pre-seed** stage; the proof will come from real deployments showing the single-engine design holds up under production workloads."
