explain: "## What the lab says\n\nThe official blog argues that the best measure of intelligence is the ability to predict the future. Reciting what is already known is relatively easy for people and models alike; the harder test is looking at the world as it is and working out where it is going. Recent advances, the post says, owe much to training models to solve problems with verifiable rewards in timeless environments. Humans learn that way too, but their most valuable lessons often come from facing a situation, deciding with only what is known in the moment, observing the outcome, and adjusting—point-in-time problems whose training signal is transient rather than timeless.\n\nThe lab’s line on today’s models is that they have “read almost everything and lived through almost nothing”: they learn from the internet all at once, already knowing how each story ends. Epochal wants models to live through thousands of yesterdays, one day at a time, the way people do. So it is building a time machine for the internet to simulate the past and teach AI to predict the future. Superhuman prediction, the company says, will redefine almost every industry, because every decision is a prediction.\n\n## First product and team\n\nOn the same day as the lab announcement, Epochal released **Epochal Time Portal**, a time-gated sandbox in which an agent can search and browse the internet exactly as it existed on a past date, then be asked to predict what happens next. It is powered by a timestamped archive and a point-in-time search index. The company says it has indexed over **a trillion** pages across **more than 30 years**, with granularity down to the **minute**. Product details are on this site’s [Epochal Time Portal](/en/terms/epochal-time-portal/) page.\n\nThe team describes itself as engineers, researchers, and product developers who believe in vertical integration, and that a small team with a singular focus can change the world. Guodong Zhang, Zihang Dai, and Manuel Kroiss are introduced as key members of xAI’s founding team.\n"
