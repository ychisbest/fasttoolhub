intro: "**Apollo World** is an **agent-driven, omni-modal world model for physical AI** first unveiled by **Baidu Apollo** at *Baidu AI Day · Apollo* in Shenzhen on **2026-11-03**, and widely covered by Xinhua, Yicai and InfoQ on Nov 4–5.\n\nRather than a pretty video generator, it acts as an **examiner** for driverless cars: in its virtual cities, cars, pedestrians and cyclists are **AI agents that make decisions and react to each other**. It generates camera frames, LiDAR point clouds and other sensor data in real time so the driving stack can perceive, decide and act as on real roads, completing full **order → pickup → drop-off** trips in closed loop. Baidu says it is already used for global testing and validation of **Apollo Go (Luobo Kuaipao)** and can simulate about 100,000 km per day."
explain: "## What problem it solves\n\nAutonomous driving has to balance **safety, intelligence and scale** at once: long-tail scenarios are hard to cover with real miles, entering a new city is costly and risky, and continuous cross-segment driving needs large-scale validation. Apollo World moves much of that testing into a virtual world first.\n\n## The \"2+3\" criteria\n\n- **2 baseline requirements**:\n  - Causality: predict the future from the past, not stitch frames together;\n  - Interactivity: respond to injected actions and change future world state.\n- **3 advanced metrics**: physical accuracy, persistence (no drift over long runs) and real-time performance (low latency).\n\n## Three core capabilities\n\n1. **Agent-driven**: traffic participants are not scripted props; they react differently when the robotaxi slows, changes lanes or yields.\n2. **Omni-modal perception**: real-time camera and LiDAR observations that change consistently with weather.\n3. **Trip-level closed loop**: full order-level simulation between arbitrary start and end points, not isolated intersection clips.\n\n## Limitations\n\n- Currently an internal Baidu stack for Apollo Go; no public API or weights.\n- Baidu says it can extend to robotics and other physical-AI settings, but there is no public product yet."
