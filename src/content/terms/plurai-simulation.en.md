intro: "**Plurai Simulation** is the AI-agent simulation and self-improvement product that Plurai officially launched on 11 November 2026. In the [official blog post](https://www.plurai.ai/blog/introducing-plurai-simulation-self-improving-layer-for-ai-agents) dated November 11, 2026, co-founder and CEO Ilan Kadar wrote: “Today we’re launching Plurai simulation, the self-improving layer for AI agents. It’s free to start for every AI engineer, and we’re announcing $13M in seed funding led by Team8.” At 14:30 UTC the same day, the company account [@pluraiAI](https://x.com/pluraiAI/status/2120524451184001519) and Kadar’s own account [@ilan_kadar](https://x.com/ilan_kadar/status/2120524450831413518) posted the launch (the company post uses the wording “self-improvement layer”) together with the seed round.\n\nOfficially, Plurai Simulation builds a digital twin of an agent and its world from the company’s own knowledge base, tools and policies, then fills a sandbox with simulated users that cover a range of intents, personas and long-tail edge cases, so policy violations, hallucinations, incorrect actions, broken tool calls, failed tasks and sensitive data leaks can be found before real customers see them. The blog says finding failures is not enough: the simulation explains why each failure happened and generates a fix, and the same simulation data is used to post-train models tailored to that agent, which the company says can raise capability while cutting the cost of running the agent at scale. In production, new failures are meant to feed back into the simulation — the official loop is simulate, fix, post-train, repeat.\n\nThe company points to a product page at [plurai.ai/simulation](https://www.plurai.ai/simulation), docs at [docs.plurai.ai](https://docs.plurai.ai/), and a starting link at [simulation.plurai.ai/connect](https://simulation.plurai.ai/connect). The docs say it works inside the coding agent you already use, with a plugin for Claude Code, Codex and GitHub Copilot; the Claude Code example is `/plugin marketplace add plurai-ai/plurai-plugins`, then `/simulate <path-to-your-agent>`. The blog also says teams can bring in agents built on enterprise platforms such as Salesforce Agentforce. A PR Newswire release (11 November 2026, 09:00 ET) announced the $13 million seed and general availability, and said access had previously been limited to selected partners and is now open to every agent builder, with a free tier.\n\nOn results, the official blog and the release say that with design partners (including Fortune 500 and major financial firms) over recent months, agent development cycles went from months to hours, failures reaching real users fell by 82%, and the cost of running reliable agents fell by 97%; in joint work with NVIDIA and Nebius, models Plurai post-trained for a global enterprise customer outperformed OpenAI and Anthropic frontier models on accuracy at about 40× and 44× lower cost, respectively. Those figures are the company’s statements; this page does not independently verify them. The seed round was led by Team8, with U&I, Velocity, Mercer and more than 20 angels and industry leaders, including CyberArk founder Udi Mokady and Reah Miyara of Google Cloud; Dr. Eyal Carmi, CTO of Microsoft 365 Copilot, joined the advisory board. The founders are Dr. Ilan Kadar and Dr. Elad Levi; the blog says they previously worked on autonomous-driving AI and with companies such as Waymo.\n\nA note on timing: the 00:32 Asia/Shanghai scan on 12 November had dropped “Plurai” as a weak official signal that was not yet confirmed as a new official name.
