intro: "**Gemini for healthcare** is a purpose-built industry solution announced on 10 November 2026 by Google Cloud. In a blog post signed by CEO Thomas Kurian, Google Cloud says it extends the Gemini agent — introduced the previous month at Gemini at Work — to health systems and health plans, so teams can delegate multi-step clinical, operational, and administrative work across the systems they already run, with human oversight. Google Cloud posted the announcement as [@googlecloud](https://x.com/googlecloud/status/2120141472636789026).\n\nThe post describes four components: skills for healthcare work (reusable, expert-written instructions for tasks such as medical-records synthesis, pre-appointment preparation, coverage and policy review, and payer benefit determination); connectors into existing clinical, financial, and administrative systems that inherit those systems' identity and role-based access controls; an open partner ecosystem and systems integrators; and a governed platform built to support HIPAA compliance, with audit logging, verifiable citations, and model choice. Official examples of connectors include read-only access to Epic EHR and Oracle Health EHR (the post says there is no write-back to the chart), plus named links for FHIR, eligibility and claims, terminology, CMS coverage, and productivity suites.\n\nGoogle Cloud says Gemini for healthcare is available in preview in the United States starting the day of the announcement. A shorter [company blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/infrastructure-and-cloud/google-cloud/gemini-for-healthcare/) the same day names launch partners including HCA Healthcare, Highmark Health, and Northwell Health. The Cloud post also quotes leaders at those and other organizations (including Optum Insight, Blue Cross Blue Shield of Massachusetts, and the American Society of Clinical Oncology). It states that customer clinical data, prompts, and generated outputs stay in the organization's governed environment and are not used to train or fine-tune Google's foundation models.\n\nHighlights:\n\n- Official Cloud blog (10 November 2026): https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/gemini-for-healthcare ;\n- Official announcement: https://x.com/googlecloud/status/2120141472636789026 ;\n- Same-day company post: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/infrastructure-and-cloud/google-cloud/gemini-for-healthcare/ ;\n- Distinct from the general **Gemini** consumer app and from frontier model names such as **Gemini 4 Carbon**. This name is the healthcare industry solution built on the Gemini agent."
