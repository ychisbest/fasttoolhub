Both are built on **Google’s Gemma 4**, for teams that want an American base model. Official blog: [Meet bison-1 and groundhog-1](https://www.baseten.co/blog/meet-bison-1-and-groundhog-1). The official account [@baseten](https://x.com/baseten) said the same day that the new models improve on **mammoth-1** and **elephant-1** in quality, keep similar speed, and add a **128k** context window plus image-and-text input.\n\nAccording to the official post, bison-1 is **#1 of 19** decision models on **Artificial Analysis Decision Index v2.1** with a score of **76**, ahead of d1.2-ultra at 75 and mammoth-1 at 71. It also has the lowest **Brier score** on the index at **0.14** (lower is better), ahead of d1.2-ultra at 0.15. The models are live on **Baseten Model APIs**, with open weights on Hugging Face. The blog says bison-1 is priced like mammoth-1."
explain: "## What problem it targets\n\nLast month Baseten released mammoth-1 and elephant-1 on **Qwen** backbones. This launch aims for similar decision quality on **Gemma 4**, an American base model, with much longer context and multimodal input. The official example is an invoice that looks ready to pay, while an earlier email shows the order was canceled — without enough history, a model can approve a payment it should reject.\n\n## Official capability and training notes\n\n- **Context**: 128k, eight times the previous decision models on Model APIs;\n- **Input**: text and images;\n- **API**: the same as the existing decision models; trying them is a model-name change;\n- **Calibration**: the blog attributes part of the Brier-score gain to the Gemma base, and part to scaling “soft labels” in the new training mixture (for example “83% answer A, 17% answer B”) instead of treating the answer as 100% certain;\n- **Pareto front**: the lab says bison-1, mammoth-1, and elephant-1 sit on the accuracy-versus-time Pareto frontier at their respective sizes.\n\n## Limits\n\nbison-1 and groundhog-1 are two models launched in the same post; do not treat groundhog-1’s expected score as bison-1’s result. Decision Index and Brier figures are the official numbers on AA Decision Index v2.1. Exact Hugging Face repo names should be taken from Baseten or Hugging Face at the time you use them."
