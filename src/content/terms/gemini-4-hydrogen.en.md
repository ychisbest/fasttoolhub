It finishes tasks 2.5x faster than 3.8 Flash and surpasses Argon on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index.” [@GeminiApp](https://x.com/GeminiApp/status/2120940601319260164) announced it at 18:03:39 UTC for fast, everyday help; the blog’s author [@koraykv](https://x.com/koraykv/status/2120941627229536511) posted the launch at 18:07:43 UTC; and [@Google](https://x.com/Google/status/2120941982004523367) followed at 18:09:08 UTC. Robby Stein, VP of Product for Google Search, posted from [@thefox](https://x.com/thefox/status/2120942632679674209) at 18:11:43 UTC that this is the biggest model upgrade to Search yet and is available now for all users globally. Companion posts include the developer blog [Gemini 4 Hydrogen: everyday coding and agentic workflows for developers](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/build-with-gemini-4-hydrogen/), the Search explainer [Google Search gets a major upgrade with Gemini 4 Hydrogen](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/gemini-4-hydrogen-search-upgrade/), and the [Gemini 4 Hydrogen model card](https://deepmind.google/models/model-cards/gemini-4-hydrogen/). It is priced flat across the entire 1 million-token context window at $1.50 per million input tokens and $7.50 per million output tokens. Versus Gemini 3.8 Flash, Google says it finishes tasks 2.5 times faster at the high default, raises Terminal-Bench 4.1 from 18.2% to 57%, and cuts fabricated claims by more than 5x. It offers four thinking levels — low, medium, high (default), and a new xhigh tier — with native 1 million-token input and up to 1 million output tokens. Google also says it is introducing Orchestrator in Google Antigravity to switch dynamically between models such as Carbon and Hydrogen in the same session; on Terminal-Bench 4.0, Google says Orchestrator reached near-Carbon task success rates while, on average, using 24% less quota and finishing 19% faster.\n\nOn availability, Google says Hydrogen is rolling out globally starting today: developers can use it in AI Studio, Android Studio, and all tiers of the Gemini API; enterprises can use it in Gemini for business, Google Antigravity, and Gemini in Google Sheets for eligible subscriptions; everyone can use it in the Gemini app model drop-down and in Google Search, with Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers also getting it in Antigravity, Gemini in Chrome, and Gemini in Sheets. Free users in the Gemini app get Hydrogen via auto model selection when it is the appropriate model.
