intro: "**d1.2** and **d1.2-ultra** are the new Liquid AI decision-model versions formally released on 16 November 2026, adding audio input and five new tasks on top of the existing **d1-ultra** line and earlier d1 models. [@liquidai](https://x.com/liquidai/status/2122346077231923698) posted the introduction at 2026-11-16T15:08:30Z (23:08:30 Beijing time), beginning “Today, we release d1.2 and d1.2-ultra, the state-of-the-art omnimodal decision models.” The post says both models can process text, images, and audio, with new output capabilities, and that d1.2-ultra scores 75, first on the @ArtificialAnlys Decision Index.\n\nThe official blog at [liquid.ai/blog/d1-2](https://www.liquid.ai/blog/d1-2) is titled “d1.2: State-of-the-Art Omnimodal Decision Models.” It describes them as new versions of Liquid’s decision models, now supporting audio input and five new tasks: reading a value from the input (text, number or date — for example, the amount due on an invoice, returning the characters it read, or “not found” instead of guessing if the value is absent); finding every passage of a kind in a text (spans, such as every piece of personal data in a note, with its position); locating things in a picture (one box, or a box for every instance plus a count); estimating a number with a range; and picking every option that applies (`choice` with `multiple` set to true). Audio can be what you ask about (for example, whether there is a siren in the background, or which language is spoken) or the question itself, spoken instead of typed.\n\nLiquid says d1.2-ultra scores 75 on the Artificial Analysis Decision Index v2.1, first among decision models and two points ahead of the previous best. The index covers decisions and extraction on résumés, receipts and research papers, tool calling, and hard knowledge questions, and also scores the probability a model assigns to each answer, so a confident wrong answer costs more than a hesitant one. The company also says both models give the best score for their cost: no model scores higher than d1.2 for less money, and no model scores higher than d1.2-ultra at any price. On calibration, it reports that across 13 benchmarks and 22,505 questions answered by all five models, d1.2-ultra’s calibration error is 0.8 points, versus 1.2 for d1.2, 2.7 for Jev 1.14, 3.7 for Perplexity’s Decider v1.3, and 7.2 for GPT-6 Luna on its Decisions API. An internal table also lists accuracy on receipt value reading, document personal-data extraction, RefCOCO localization, COCO counting and multi-select, and ESC-50 / FLEURS audio questions; those figures are company results on held-out splits of public datasets.\n\nBoth models are available today on the Liquid API as `d1.2` and `d1.2-ultra`, with a key from console.liquid.ai, at $0.02 and $0.042 per million input tokens respectively, with input-only billing. Liquid’s example: a 1024×1024 image costs 1.5 tokens per 32×32-pixel patch, or 1,536 tokens; a request bills its text and pictures once, and each extra question adds only its own text. The company says the models are coming to OpenRouter and Vercel soon, without a firm date.\n\nKey points:\n\n- Official introduction: https://x.com/liquidai/status/2122346077231923698 ; official blog: https://www.liquid.ai/blog/d1-2 ;\n- These are **concrete new model names in Liquid AI’s decision-model line**, not the d1-ultra already stored on 17 October 2026, and not the earlier term LiquidAI d1-3B.
