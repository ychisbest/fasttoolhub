intro: "**RoboFinals RL-Env** is the reinforcement-learning simulation environment Lightwheel introduced on 11 November 2026. Officially it is the training side of the existing RoboFinals evaluation platform: the same tasks, scenes and physics, now available to train policies that are then scored on the same task definitions. The official account [@LightwheelAI](https://x.com/LightwheelAI/status/2120350216679493836) posted at 2026-11-11T02:57:40Z. The product page is [lightwheel.ai/robofinals-rl-env](https://lightwheel.ai/robofinals-rl-env). RoboFinals the benchmark is older (Lightwheel’s introduction page has dated that launch to 4 December 2025); this entry records the new training-environment name.\n\nOn the official page, RL in simulation usually gets two things wrong: the task is invented by a researcher rather than taken from real work, and the physics is tuned until it looks right rather than measured. RoboFinals RL-Env tries to close both gaps. The pipeline is: record a person doing a real industrial task first-person with EgoSuite; have agents parse sub-goals, objects and order; rebuild the scene as SimReady OpenUSD assets; calibrate with measurements of the real parts (friction, stiffness, damping and so on) and industrial-grade CAE checks on deformable response; then assemble a trainable environment on a Newton-native backend. The page says contact among rigid, deformable and granular bodies is resolved by coupled solvers in a single timestep. Agents do the construction at each stage; simulation engineers review before a task is released.\n\nThe first released task is automotive repair. Officially the same environment is hard in three ways that matter for physical AI: long-horizon, multi-stage work that runs for minutes with later stages depending on earlier ones; a dexterous-hand embodiment rather than a parallel gripper; and hoses, cables and gaskets modeled as deformables. As EgoSuite captures grow, Lightwheel says agents will keep building environments along one taxonomy (scene, operation and difficulty, asset type, task horizon), with experts reviewing, so the library tracks the distribution of real work rather than an editorial list. Early access is open to teams training manipulation policies. The full evaluation benchmark and a public leaderboard are said to open later this year, and policies trained here are evaluated on the same task definitions.\n\nKey links:\n\n- Official product page: https://lightwheel.ai/robofinals-rl-env ;\n- Announcement post (2026-11-11T02:57:40Z): https://x.com/LightwheelAI/status/2120350216679493836 ;\n- RoboFinals the benchmark: https://lightwheel.ai/robofinals ;\n- This is a **new name for a training environment**. Do not collapse it with the older evaluation platform **RoboFinals**, or with Lightwheel’s EgoSuite or SimReady assets."
