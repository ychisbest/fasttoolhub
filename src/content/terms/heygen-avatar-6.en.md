explain: "HeyGen describes Avatar 6 as audio-driven and context-aware: it matches mouth movement to speech and also performs gestures suggested by the script. The research page shows action cues written next to lines (a thumbs-up on “Hello,” pointing when the script mentions a counter), performed in that person’s style on cue. Identity consistency is another official claim: footage of the person is used to learn how they look and move. The press release says an avatar can be created from as little as about 30 seconds of video, with optional extra footage.\n\nResolution and duration are the other advertised limits. The research page says every frame can be generated at up to 2880×1620 and shows continuous 25–28 minute clips from a single audio track, without looping. Official comparisons against other commercial avatar products claim first place on multiple criteria and overall score, including Avatar Arena voting.\n\nThe launch materials also stress consent: third-party platforms may generate photo-based or virtual avatars, while realistic videos of real people remain on HeyGen’s platform and require explicit consent. Availability, pricing, and API limits follow the product and docs at the time you use them. Do not treat Avatar 6 as the same name as earlier HeyGen avatar generations or another vendor’s avatar model."
