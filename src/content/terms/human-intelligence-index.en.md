intro: "The **Human Intelligence Index** (**HII**) is a model ranking **DesignArena** launched on 23 November 2026 to score AI models by their impact on the human experience, rather than by the economic value they create. The official account [@DesignArena](https://x.com/DesignArena/status/2124943628707713372) posted “Introducing the Human Intelligence Index (HII)” at 19:10 UTC, pointing to [designarena.ai/blog/human-intelligence-index](https://www.designarena.ai/blog/human-intelligence-index) and the [leaderboard](https://www.designarena.ai/leaderboard).\n\nDesignArena says the index is weighted by how Americans actually spend their time: **624 billion hours** a year across apps, games, the web, and productivity tools. First results were published in the launch post.\n"
explain: "## What it is trying to measure\n\nDesignArena already compares models’ design ability through community battles and human judgment. HII extends that idea to the experience value models add in the places people actually spend time. The official contrast is with common economic or productivity benchmarks: those measure value created for the economy, while HII measures value created for people, weighted by U.S. time-use data. The exact task mix, weighting formula, and refresh cadence are defined on the live methodology and leaderboard pages.\n\n## First official results\n\nIn the introduction post, [@DesignArena](https://x.com/DesignArena/status/2124943628707713372) reported these initial scores (out of 100):\n\n- First: **Claude Fable 5.2**, **54/100**\n- Second: **Claude Opus 5.5**, **50/100**\n- Third-place tie: **Gemini 4 Carbon** and **GPT-6.2 Astra**, both **47/100**\n\nThese are the organizer’s launch-day rankings and will change as the leaderboard updates. They are not an absolute, cross-benchmark ability score. For current standings, use the [DesignArena leaderboard](https://www.designarena.ai/leaderboard).\n"
