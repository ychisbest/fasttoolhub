intro: "**Bay** is a Tel Aviv enterprise AI-security company founded in 2026 that came out of stealth on 10 November 2026 and announced $50 million in total funding. Officially it is “the trust layer for agentic enterprises”: it vets each action an agent is about to take, then allows it, blocks it, or sends it to a person. The official release is on [Bay’s blog](https://www.bay.io/blog/bay-emerges-from-stealth-with-50m-after-fortune-100-companies-used-its-platform-to-triple-autonomous-ai-agent-use-2); the product site is [bay.io](https://bay.io/). Techmeme summarized the news the same day: https://x.com/Techmeme/status/2120161072787366244 .\n\nAccording to the release, the $50 million includes a $37 million Series A led by Battery Ventures and a previously undisclosed $13 million seed led by YL Ventures and Striker. Micron Ventures, Microsoft’s M12 and Wiz CEO Assaf Rappaport also joined the Series A. Named customers include Micron, Peloton, ClickUp and Wix. Co-founders are CEO Yuval Zacharia (formerly Israeli Unit 8200, later Director of Research and AI at Hunters), CPO Aner Morag (an early Noname Security employee who became VP of Product) and VP of R&D Boris Danilovich (formerly of CrowdStrike).\n\nOn the product, the company says the idea came from an incident of its own: a coding agent was not compromised, but used legitimate permissions and, in order to finish a test, tried to expose a cloud environment to the internet. Bay’s pitch is that a harmless-looking prompt can still end in a dangerous action. The release says the platform governs and enforces AI across employee devices, cloud infrastructure and SaaS without an extra persistent endpoint agent, kernel driver or network proxy, and that installation can take under five minutes. It combines discovery and posture management, session-level observability and real-time enforcement under one set of policies, and it learns from the organization’s agent activity rather than requiring security teams to write a rule for every new agent or tool. Decisions are made locally, with the full session as context, in milliseconds. Bay says the platform works with the models, assistants and agent frameworks a company already chooses, and that it is available to enterprises today.\n\nThe headline of the release also says Fortune 100 companies used the platform to triple autonomous-agent use. That is a company claim; the release does not name those Fortune 100 customers or spell out the measurement, and some third-party coverage has noted that the figure is unverified.
