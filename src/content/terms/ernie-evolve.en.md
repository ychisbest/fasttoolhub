intro: "**ERNIE Evolve** is Baidu ERNIE's **automated research system**, first described on 9 November 2026 — a concrete step toward putting recursive self-improvement (RSI) into real R&D. Given a clear goal and constraints, it proposes research directions, edits code, runs experiments and stores what it learned; only improvements that pass **independent evaluation** and **review** are kept.\n\nIt runs two nested loops. The outer loop uses past results and a knowledge base to pick new directions and run several research lanes in parallel; the inner loop keeps editing, evaluating and writing up experience inside one direction. Roles are split across agents: a **Director** chooses the next direction, a **Selector** picks the starting code and hypothesis, an **Executor** edits code in an isolated environment, an **Evaluator** scores the run with a fixed procedure the agents cannot see, and a **Reviewer** checks for goal drift or eval gaming.\n\nHighlights:\n\n- Introduced on 2026-11-09 on the ERNIE site and by Baidu's official account @Baidu_Inc;\n- Reported publicly verifiable state-of-the-art numbers on NanoChat AutoResearch and NanoGPT Speedrun, with the optimization artifacts open-sourced;\n- Already used on upcoming ERNIE 5.5 Flash inference work, including a Kunlunxin P800 low-latency path (~168 hours, generation speed to 623.6% of baseline), deterministic-inference throughput (~3 days, 133.7% of baseline) and a decode-speed pass (114.9% of baseline at matching accuracy);\n- No public API or self-serve product yet — it is aimed at research and infra teams working on training, inference and kernel optimization;\n- Write-up: https://ernie.baidu.com/blog/ernie-evolve-toward-automated-research-for-real-world-ai-rd ."
