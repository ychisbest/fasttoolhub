intro: "**PLaMo 3 Translate** is a **translation-specialized LLM** from Japan's **Preferred Networks (PFN)** and the model behind its commercial *PLaMo Translation* service. On **2026-11-05** PFN published **open weights** for all four sizes on Hugging Face (`pfnet`): **0.8B, 2B, 8B and 31B**.\n\nIt is built on **PLaMo 3 NICT**, a base model co-developed by PFN and Japan's NICT, further trained on tens of billions of translation tokens. It translates among **53 languages**, accepts surrounding context and glossaries, and lets you choose plain or polite style for Japanese output. PFN says even the 0.8B model beats translation-specialized LLMs more than 30× its size on Japanese–English and Japanese–other-language pairs."
explain: "## What problem it solves\n\nConfidential documents (contracts, medical records, internal material) often can't be sent to a cloud translation API. PLaMo 3 Translate lets companies and individuals translate locally on **phones, laptops and on-prem servers**, even offline.\n\n## Sizes and formats\n\n| Size | Typical use |\n|---|---|\n| 0.8B / 2B | On-device translation on phones and light laptops |\n| 8B | High-quality local translation on one GPU or a Mac |\n| 31B | Highest-quality server-side translation |\n\nEach size ships in **BF16, FP8, W4A16, W4AFP8, NVFP4, GGUF and MLX-4bit**, runnable with Transformers, vLLM, llama.cpp or mlx-lm. Max training length was 32,768 tokens for input plus output.\n\n## How it was built\n\nTournament-based data synthesis, back-translation and iterative self-distillation, combined with SFT, DPO and distillation into the smaller sizes.\n\n## License and limits\n\n- **PLaMo Community License**: free for personal and non-commercial (including academic) use; companies with ≤ JPY 1 billion revenue in the last fiscal year can use it commercially for free after registering; larger companies need a separate license.\n- Translation-only; not meant as a general chat model.\n- Split very long texts so input plus output stays within the training length."
usage: "## Who it's for\n\n- Companies that need high-quality Japanese↔English/Chinese translation without data leaving their network;\n- Mobile developers embedding offline translation in apps;\n- Localization, subtitling and document-translation professionals.\n\n## Getting started\n\n1. Read PFN's [tech blog](https://www.preferred.jp/en/blog/tech/plamo-3-translate-weight-release) and pick a size.\n2. Mac: `mlx_lm.generate --model pfnet/plamo-3-translate-8b-MLX-4bit`; PC: load `pfnet/plamo-3-translate-8b-GGUF` in llama.cpp.\n3. Server: `vllm serve pfnet/plamo-3-translate-31b-FP8`.\n4. Follow the model card's prompt format for source/target language, context and glossary; check registration or licensing before commercial use."
