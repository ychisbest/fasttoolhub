explain: "## Training idea and interface\n\nFollow-up posts and the technical report describe three training objectives used together: pairwise cross-modal alignment, structured random-feature prediction (RFP), and masked waveform reconstruction. A shared, signal-conditioned encoder processes each physical channel independently and can take different numbers and combinations of signals. The published assets are representations only; task-specific prediction heads are not included.\n\nThe model card releases the encoder weights and inference code under the **MIT** license. Inputs can be `[T]`, `[C,T]`, `[B,C,T]`, or aligned channel dicts; you must supply the true sampling rate and physical channel identities. The code package resamples to a fixed 128 Hz and handles observed-sample and storage-padding masks. CLS and observed-valid PatchMean are returned separately as `[B, C*256]`, with channels concatenated in input order. `return_tokens=True` also returns ordered patch features and validity masks at 0.25-second intervals.\n\n## Officially reported metrics\n\nIn the introduction thread, Ant Ling reports that the frozen backbone:\n\n- cuts **PPG-DaLiA** heart-rate error by **25.1%** versus AnyPPG\n- reaches Macro-F1 **0.5890** on **PTB-XL** rhythm classification\n- reaches Macro-F1 **0.6186** on **EEGMAT** mental arithmetic\n- in seven sleep-staging setups from multimodal to single-sensor, reaches Macro-F1 **0.7784** on **HMC** and **0.7941** on **Sleep-EDF20**\n- reaches AUROC **0.9183** for single-lead apnea screening on **Apnea-ECG**\n\nThese figures come from the vendor thread and paper; they are not a clinical or production SLA. The paper is titled *LingSante-Pulse: Unified Physiological Pretraining through Random-Feature Prediction and Cross-Modal Alignment*.\n"
