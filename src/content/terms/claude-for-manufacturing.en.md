intro: "**Claude for Manufacturing** is the manufacturing plugin Anthropic officially launched on 12 November 2026. In the official article [Introducing Claude for Manufacturing](https://claude.com/resources/articles/claude-for-manufacturing) (datePublished 2026-11-12), the company wrote: “Today we’re launching Claude for Manufacturing, a plugin that includes skills for work from pricing a new job to diagnosing a stopped machine.” The deck says it is a new plugin with 17 skills and four connectors that helps manufacturers draft quotes, quality reports, and supplier requests from the systems they already run.\n\nOfficially, an estimator can have Claude read a customer’s request, compare costs on similar jobs, and draft the quote; a quality engineer can document a failed inspection, check past reports, rank likely causes, and draft a corrective-action report; a technician can combine sensor exports, repair notes, photos, and a manual to rank likely causes; a buyer can prepare a request for quotes and compare bids; an engineer can use the Autodesk Fusion connector to generate variations from an existing model, score them against targets, and draft a decision memo; and a plant manager can have Claude compile output, downtime, and the cost of missed production. The 17 skills cover sales, quality, operations, supply chain, design, and setup. Officially, they are a starting point: organizations can extend them, and Anthropic plans to add more manufacturing skills over time.\n\nThe official connectors are Autodesk Fusion, Infor, Microsoft 365, and Oracle NetSuite; the company says that software stays central to how teams run the business. For systems with no connector, teams can save regular exports to Microsoft 365 and have Claude work from those files. Infor is also releasing its own plugin: the Infor connector in Claude for Manufacturing is for lookups, while Infor’s plugin can, after approval and within the user’s Infor permissions, make changes such as releasing a purchase order or rescheduling production. Officially, skills are built to end with a responsible person’s review, and Claude can link figures and claims back to the underlying data. Claude for Manufacturing does not operate equipment. The article also says Anthropic is developing the Model Hardware Standard, a research preview that would give programmable equipment a standard way to describe measurements, settings, and safety limits — that is not this plugin itself.\n\nTo get started, official instructions say to install the [plugin](https://claude.com/marketplace/plugins/claude-for-manufacturing) on Team or Enterprise plans and tell Claude, “Set us up.” Claude asks which systems the team uses, tests the connectors, and reports which skills cannot fully run yet. Anthropic also scheduled a technical webinar for 17 November at 11:00 a.m. ET. Public coverage from Manufacturing Dive and Business Insider reported the launch on 12 November; an early X mention was @financialjuice at 2026-11-12T16:57:13Z.
