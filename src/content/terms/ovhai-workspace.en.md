A French version is at [OVHai Workspace la plateforme agentique pour les entreprises](https://corporate.ovhcloud.com/fr/newsroom/news/ovhai-workspace-agentic-platform-business/).\n\nThe release says the platform “puts the full power of AI just a prompt away” and provides three spaces: **Productivity**, which brings together email, online storage, video conferencing, and AI assistants; **Web AI**, born from the transformation of the Web Cloud offering and dedicated to creating and managing websites; and **Knowledge Platform**, a search engine designed for both users and AI agents, to make company data easier to access. It states that the alpha version of the Productivity space is available free of charge from today, with 5 GB of storage per user, and that data is hosted in Europe and is not used to train models.\n\nThis entry is for the product name **OVHai Workspace**. CEO Octave Klaba added that OVHai Workspace will also be available as a Private Tenant for a single customer and as an on-premises appliance with integrated GPUs."
explain: "Officially, **OVHai Workspace** is a B2B agentic platform for businesses, not a single chat box. The press release splits it into three spaces.\n\n**Productivity** is the collaborative space. It gathers email, calendar, document editing, storage, and video conferencing around a single AI. The release says a simple prompt is enough to organise a meeting or find a piece of information. The alpha is available free of charge from the announcement day, with 5 GB of storage per user; data is hosted in Europe and is not used to train models. This is the only part of the launch that is explicitly described as usable today.\n\n**Web AI** comes from the transformation of OVHcloud’s Web Cloud offering and is dedicated to creating and managing websites. The first step is **Website Builder**: a few sentences of description are enough to generate a complete website, with hosting, domain name, SSL certificate, email, and backups included. The release says the same approach will later extend to applications, while AI agents will take care of site updates, security, and visibility in search engines (SEO) and in AI answer engines (GEO).\n\n**Knowledge Platform** ships first as a search engine for both users and AI agents. OVHcloud says it is built on patented R&D and combines semantic and lexical search across hundreds of millions of documents per collection. Later, the Knowledge Platform is meant to help businesses bring together, govern, and connect scattered data, and to look at it in a new way with ontologies.\n\nOn go-to-market, the release says OVH will relaunch **OVH.com** with this agentic experience for businesses, while **OVHcloud.com** is now refocused on developers. The group aims to reach several tens of millions of customers, starting with its web-hosting base. CEO Octave Klaba said the platform is meant to make it simple to govern employees’ use of AI, the activity of AI agents, and access to data, “in a secure environment and under full control thanks to observability.” He also said OVHai Workspace will be available as a **Private Tenant** for a single customer and as an on-premises **appliance** with integrated GPUs."
