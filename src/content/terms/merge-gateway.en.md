It sits alongside **Merge Unified** (data integrations) and **Merge Agent Handler** (agent tools) in Merge’s stack.\n\nPurpose: access major LLMs through **one API**, with **routing**, **cost controls**, **failover**, and security features, instead of juggling separate provider accounts and invoices.\n\nHighlights (from Merge’s product/docs pages):\n\n- One endpoint / key for models across vendors; new models Merge adds become available without new integrations;\n- Route by cost, latency, or quality; **automatic failover** when a provider is down;\n- Budgets and spend attribution by project/team/customer; optional context compression;\n- Policies, DLP / prompt-injection controls, and logging of routing decisions;\n- Integrate by pointing SDKs at Gateway base URLs (e.g. OpenAI-compatible `https://api-gateway.merge.dev/v1`) or using the Merge Gateway SDK."
explain: "## Problem\n\nProduction AI products often hard-code one vendor, then face outages, price swings, and multi-invoice ops. Teams also need per-customer budgets and compliance filters without building a custom gateway.\n\n## Design\n\n- **Model aliases & routing policies**: send `@alias/...` instead of a hard-coded model ID; edit the policy to change failover/cost/quality targets without redeploying;\n- **Projects**: separate spend, budgets, and policies by team or environment;\n- **Customer controls**: embed per-tenant routing, BYOK, and usage limits in your product;\n- Wire formats for OpenAI, Anthropic, Vercel AI SDK, and LangChain via documented base URLs.\n\n## Limits & notes\n\n- Pricing/plans (Free vs Pro catalog access) are defined by Merge—check current Plans and pricing on docs;\n- Exact model catalog and supported vendors change over time; use Merge’s model catalog rather than inventing lists;\n- Distinct from Merge Agent Handler (tools) and Merge Unified (SaaS data sync)."
usage: "## Who it's for\n\n- Product teams shipping multi-model AI features who need one integration surface;\n- Platforms that must offer customers routing policies, budgets, or BYOK;\n- Engineering orgs consolidating LLM spend and failover across vendors.\n\n## Getting started\n\n1. Read https://www.merge.dev/gateway and https://docs.merge.dev/merge-gateway/get-started ;\n2. For existing OpenAI/Anthropic/LangChain clients, change the base URL to the matching Gateway endpoint from the docs table;\n4. Create a routing policy / model alias for production traffic instead of pinning a single vendor model ID."
