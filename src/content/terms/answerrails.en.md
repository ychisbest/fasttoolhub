Replies must be **grounded in your published pages** with clickable citations; if the corpus cannot support an answer, it **refuses** and offers a human handoff instead of inventing one.\n\nIt is **bring-your-own-key** (OpenAI, Anthropic, or Mistral)—traffic goes to your provider account, not through AnswerRails servers. Free tier includes the full assistant plus privacy machinery; Pro adds catalog/web sources and operator leverage. New installs can start on a free Mistral key."
