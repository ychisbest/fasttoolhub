Internal scores listed include 64.6 on DeepSWE 1.1 (versus 58.7 for Qwen3.8-Flash-Next and 54.4 for DeepSeek-V4-Flash-0731), 86.52 on Terminal-Bench 2.1 (versus 78.2 for Claude-Opus-4.6 Max), 90.4 on GPQA-Diamond, 80.6 on IFBench, and 79.3 on AA-LCR; Edge0-120B is listed in the same table at 53.6, 81.3, 87.4, 79.3, and 81.0. The [Edge0-AI/Edge0](https://github.com/Edge0-AI/Edge0) repository describes the 145B / 120B line as latent MoE models derived from Qwen3.8-Flash-Next, trained natively at 4 bits, with Q3_K and NVFP4 weights, running through `edge0-infer serve` / `chat` on Linux or Windows with an NVIDIA GPU and CUDA 13.
