intro: "**PopAgent** is an Apple-platform agent tool that indie Mac developer Tualatrix ([@tualatrix](https://x.com/tualatrix)) officially launched on 26 November 2026, starting with a macOS build. In his [launch post](https://x.com/tualatrix/status/2125836921549340977) he wrote that he had spent 150 days and nights building it, that it was “finally officially released today,” and that an iOS version would follow soon. The product site is [popagent.ai](https://popagent.ai/), with an English page at [popagent.ai/en](https://popagent.ai/en/). The post went out at 06:19 UTC on 26 November 2026 (14:19 Beijing time).\n\nIn the same post he positioned PopAgent for people who find ChatGPT bloated, do not want to be locked to a single vendor, or want to use other agents through one agent. He said it uses a fully in-house stack, that the installer is only 25 MB, and that it runs smoothly on an 8 GB MacBook Neo. He also called the 1.0 release a starting point and mentioned a launch discount. The feature and pricing details below come from the website and that post; they are the vendor’s description.\n"
explain: "## What the company claims\n\nThe account below comes from the 26 November [launch post](https://x.com/tualatrix/status/2125836921549340977) and the site [popagent.ai](https://popagent.ai/). It is the vendor’s description.\n\nThe English homepage is titled “The agent native to Mac.” The site says PopAgent waits in a corner of the screen and pops up when you click it, and that you can drop a PDF, a screenshot, a web link, or some text on it and ask about it. It also says PopAgent can see a task through on its own—reading and organising files, researching on the web, and writing and editing documents—and that it can run Claude Code, Codex, and Antigravity so you can keep the tools you already use and compare their results session by session.\n\n### Models and billing\n\nThe site says you can bring your own subscription or API key: sign in with a ChatGPT, Grok, or Kimi plan you already pay for, use a Claude plan through Claude Code, or add an API key. The listed connectors include Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, xAI, DeepSeek, Mistral, Moonshot AI, Z.ai, MiniMax, OpenRouter, Groq, Cerebras, Fireworks, Together, Hugging Face, Amazon Bedrock, Google Vertex, Azure OpenAI, GitHub Copilot, Vercel AI Gateway, Cloudflare, plus Ollama, LM Studio, and any OpenAI-compatible server. The company says you do not pay PopAgent for model usage: your chosen provider bills you directly, and PopAgent does not resell tokens or take a cut.\n\n### Local-first design and size\n\nThe site stresses a local-first, privacy-first design: sessions, files, and credentials stay on the Mac; prompts go straight to the AI provider you choose, never through PopAgent’s servers; and popagent.ai handles updates and the licence. It says the app is built only for Mac with Apple’s own technologies (Swift, AppKit, and SwiftUI) rather than a cross-platform framework. The published measurements are about 90 MB of memory idle with one window, about 140 MB with three sessions running, and about 70 MB on disk for the app, execution host, and workers. The launch post separately says the installer is only 25 MB and that the app runs smoothly on an 8 GB MacBook Neo. Those two size figures come from different vendor pages and are recorded as such.\n\n### How it works\n\nThe site also lists: every session is saved locally, and a task can get its own git worktree so parallel sessions do not collide; you can connect MCP servers, including ones that sign in with OAuth, and add tools through extensions; you can schedule a prompt to run on its own and come back to the result; you can pair an iPhone or Apple Watch to follow a session away from the desk; Automations, MCP servers, and Skills can follow you to other Macs through iCloud, while each Mac decides what to switch on; and you can see every skill PopAgent loads, turn any of them off, and review a new skill’s files and scripts before it installs.\n\nThese details are the company’s own description, not a third-party reproduction.\n"
usage: "## How to try it\n\nAccording to the website and the launch post, the macOS app is available first, from [popagent.ai](https://popagent.ai/). The site says every paid feature is open for 7 days with no card required; when the trial ends, PopAgent switches to a free tier that runs one session at a time, and every session you started stays yours. A licence is a one-time purchase that includes one year of feature updates. After that you can optionally pay for another year of updates; nothing happens if you do not. The launch prices listed on the site are $21 for Basic (1 Mac), marked down from $30, with optional $15-a-year update renewal, and $56 for Team, marked down from $80. The launch post also mentioned a first-day discount.\n\nModel usage requires your own subscription or API key and is billed by that provider.
