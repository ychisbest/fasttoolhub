intro: "**ChatGPT Wallet** is the agent payment wallet OpenAI formally introduced on 13 November 2026. The official post [Introducing ChatGPT Wallet](https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-wallet) describes it as the first wallet that lets agents pay without access to users’ actual card details and that requires a security check for every purchase, while preserving existing card rewards and protections for eligible disputes. It is built with American Express, Mastercard, and Visa, and first becomes available through dots for ChatGPT Pro subscribers in the US.\n\nOpenAI’s purchase flow is: a user adds a card once, then before each purchase reviews the items, total, and selected card and approves with a security check such as Face ID or a fingerprint. After approval, the payment network issues a payment token — a substitute number, security code, and expiration date — and the charge goes to the selected card without sharing the actual card details with the business or the agent. Users still transact directly with businesses, which keep pricing, fulfillment, returns, and customer service. OpenAI also says it later expects users to pre-approve some purchases within spending limits, categories, or time windows; the current launch still requires a check on every purchase.\n\nAlongside the wallet, OpenAI published a [Commerce Partner Playbook](https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-wallet) with guidance for businesses on agent access (including recognizing requests signed with Web Bot Auth), product information, and working with risk and payment providers so legitimate agent purchases are not declined by mistake. The Help Center article [Making purchases with ChatGPT Wallet](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001654-making-purchases-with-chatgpt-wallet) adds availability limits: Wallet works in conversations with a dot, and Pro users should set up a dot first; it is not available in Chat, Work, or standalone Codex threads; approving a purchase requires biometric verification on a supported device; and it is for users aged 18 or older who live in the United States, have a US billing address, and hold an eligible US-issued American Express, Chase, Mastercard, or Visa card.\n\n[CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/openai-chatgpt-wallet-dot-purchases.html) reported the same launch at 10:00 AM EST on 13 November 2026 (23:00 Beijing time).
