Hedra is an a16z-backed research company in San Francisco; its founder and CEO is Michael Lingelbach. The official account [@hedra_labs](https://x.com/hedra_labs/status/2120207555263905973) posted at 2026-11-10T17:30:47Z. A [GlobeNewswire release](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/10/3399821/0/en/hedra-launches-model-factory-an-autonomous-post-training-and-inference-platform-for-real-world-ai.html) dated 10:00 ET the same day describes the product and says it is available at [hedra.com](https://hedra.com/).\n\nIn the official materials, general-purpose models often do not know a particular robot, factory line or lab. Model Factory takes the recursive self-improvement loop that has mostly lived inside a few frontier labs and offers it to teams that bring their own simulators and data. The release splits the loop into four stages: compute (GPU sandboxes on Hedra’s inference engine; the company stresses there is no long-term GPU commitment), post-training (customers bring simulation engines and data; Hedra researchers work with autonomous agents to build RL environments and datasets, and can also help with data capture, evals and the training-to-serving loop when a customer has no research team), deploy (low-latency serving on premises or at a nearby partner data center — examples include distilling world action models to run faster than real time, or fitting a vision-language-action model on an edge device; existing models can also be served on Hedra’s engine), and self-improve (after deployment, models are retrained from production performance and only versions that pass the customer’s evals are released).\n\nThe release also cites company-reported research numbers: a physically retrained H3 scored 34.9 on Google DeepMind’s Physics-IQ, versus 30.3 for H3 Max, with comparable visual quality claimed; an autonomously distilled version rendered a five-second clip in under three seconds on AMD MI355X GPUs. On image editing, Hedra said it cut 2K edits from about 22 seconds (original bf16, 30 steps) to 3.6 seconds (a distilled 14-step model) on the same four MI355X GPUs in under a week, roughly 6×. Hedra also said revenue grew 15× over the past 12 months.
