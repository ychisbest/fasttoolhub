intro: "**Worken** is a **framework proposed by AGIBOT (智元机器人) for measuring how much productive work robots actually do** in real environments. It was introduced at **AGIBOT Innovation Day 2026** in Shanghai on **October 29, 2026**. The idea is to move the industry's headline number from \"robots deployed\" to work done autonomously.\n\nAnnouncement: [AGIBOT on X](https://x.com/AGIBOTofficial/status/2115694835579666505) · Chinese post: [AGIBOT Hong Kong](https://x.com/AGIBOTHongkong/status/2115700858675683691)"
explain: "## Key points\n\n1. **The formula**: as summarized by attendees, Worken = **active robots × autonomous hours × output efficiency**. A robot that sits idle or needs constant human takeover scores low even if it was sold.\n2. **Why a new metric**: embodied AI companies often report shipments or deployments. AGIBOT argues buyers in factories and warehouses care about reliable, measurable output.\n3. **Launched alongside**: the same event introduced **G2 Max** (industrial material-handling robot, dual arms up to 40 kg), **AHA3** humanoid hardware, **GO-3 Preview** foundation model, **WITA-Omni 1.0** interaction model and **Link-U OS 1.0**.\n\n## Status\n\nAGIBOT calls Worken a *proposed* framework, so the exact definitions and whether other robot makers adopt it are still open."
usage: "## How to apply the idea\n\n- **Track three numbers per robot fleet**: how many robots are actively working, how many hours they run without human intervention, and output per hour versus a human or legacy baseline.\n- **Compare vendors** on Worken-style output rather than unit counts or demo videos.\n- **Watch AGIBOT's channels** for the formal definition and any published Worken figures from customer sites."
