intro: "**Ampersand Tutor** (written **&Tutor**) is a tutoring model released by the AI lab **humans&** on **October 30, 2026**. Instead of being an all-purpose assistant, it is trained specifically to **teach** — to help a learner actually understand a problem rather than simply hand over the answer.\n\nIts distinctive training trick: humans& first built **Persimmon**, a large \"user model\" that simulates how real people behave and learn, and then trained &Tutor with reinforcement learning to teach Persimmon. The reward is how much the *simulated student learns*. &Tutor is available through the humans& agent API as `ampersand-tutor-v0.1`."
explain: "## Key facts\n\n- **Developer**: humans& (an AI research startup focused on human-centric models).\n- **Base model**: built on **GLM-5.3**, a mixture-of-experts transformer (78 layers, 256 routed experts + 1 shared, 8 active per token), served in NVFP4.\n- **Modality**: text in, text out; evaluated with a 131K-token context.\n- **Training**: RL in simulated tutoring conversations. **Persimmon** plays the learner; a judge measures the learner's learning gain. There are no hand-written rubrics for \"scaffolding\" or \"don't give away the answer\" — those behaviours emerge from the objective.\n- **Results (vendor-reported)**: on the **TutorMoments** benchmark, &Tutor scores **0.754** with a plain prompt vs **0.500** for its GLM-5.3 starting point, ahead of Claude Sonnet 5.5 (0.667) and GPT-6 Astra (0.634). With an evaluation-aware prompt it scores 0.919, just behind GPT-6 Astra (0.926), and has the best \"appropriate rigor\" score.\n- **Ablation**: training against GLM-5.3 as the simulated learner instead of Persimmon performed significantly worse, which humans& uses to argue that a better model of people yields a better teacher.\n\n## Caveats\n\nAll scores come from simulated students and LLM judges, not measured outcomes with real learners. humans& itself notes the results may not generalize to real students, other subjects or long-term learning."
