intro: "**Godwit** is an **open-source, AI-native desktop email client** for macOS, Windows and Linux, developed by Baran Wang and published on GitHub as `godwit-email/godwit`. It is named after the bar-tailed godwit, the bird that holds the record for the longest non-stop flight.\n\nGodwit works with **any IMAP/SMTP account** and ships with an assistant called **Wit** that reads, summarises, translates and drafts email using **the model you choose** – any OpenAI- or Anthropic-compatible API, or local models via Ollama and LM Studio. Version **1.21.0** shipped on **October 30, 2026**, and the app launched on **Product Hunt on October 31, 2026**."
explain: "## Main features\n\n- **Mail**: IMAP/SMTP sync, offline changes, conversation view, full-text search and HTML compose; auto-detected setup for Gmail, Outlook, iCloud, Yahoo, QQ Mail, 163/126, Lark/Feishu, DingTalk and more.\n- **Wit assistant**: answers questions about your mailbox, reads attachments (PDF, Office, CSV) and drafts replies – it **never sends without your approval**.\n- **AI actions**: summarise, translate, change tone/length and one-tap quick replies on a message, selection or draft; sign-in codes surfaced with one click.\n- **Bring your own model**: OpenAI-compatible (Chat Completions or Responses) or Anthropic-compatible endpoints, including local Ollama / LM Studio. API keys are stored in the OS keychain.\n- **Extensions**: drop in a `SKILL.md` to add a button; TypeScript plugins with declared permissions add cards, actions and Wit tools; MCP servers give Wit external tools.\n- **`godwit mcp`**: an MCP server that lets Claude Code, Cursor, Codex and other agents **read mail and create drafts, but never send**.\n\n## Privacy model\n\nNo Godwit account, no telemetry and no relay server: mail, drafts and the search index stay on your computer, passwords stay in the keychain, and the only Godwit-owned host contacted is the update server."
usage: "## Getting started\n\n1. Add your mailbox – common providers are auto-configured over IMAP/SMTP.\n3. In settings, point Wit at a model: an OpenAI/Anthropic-compatible API key, or a local endpoint such as `localhost:11434` (Ollama).\n4. Optional: enable `godwit mcp` and connect it to Claude Code, Cursor or Codex so your coding agent can search mail and prepare drafts.\n\n## Good fits\n\n- Developers who want an inbox their AI agents can read without giving them send rights\n- Privacy-sensitive users who want AI email help with local models\n- Teams that want to extend their mail client with custom skills and plugins"
