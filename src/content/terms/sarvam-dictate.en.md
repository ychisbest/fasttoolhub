intro: "**Sarvam Dictate** is a dictation API that **Sarvam AI** released on 23 November 2026 for developers adding speech-to-intended-text to their apps, in English and **22 Indian languages**. The official account [@SarvamAI](https://x.com/SarvamAI/status/2124893117069570473) announced it at 15:49 UTC, alongside the product post: [sarvam.ai/blogs/sarvam-dictate](https://www.sarvam.ai/blogs/sarvam-dictate).\n\nThe pipeline has two stages: **Saaras V4** transcribes the audio, then a dictation-tuned **Sarvam 105B** revises the transcript toward the text the speaker meant to write. The company argues that people do not speak the way they type—they repeat themselves, restart sentences, and use names a speech model may never have seen—and that Dictate is built for that, not for verbatim transcript alone.\n"
explain: "## What the company claims\n\nThe blog and official thread highlight:\n\n- **Edit rate**: on real dictations up to a minute long, Sarvam Dictate needs **40%** fewer character edits than **Gemini 3.5 Transcribe** in English, and **47%** fewer across Indian languages and Hinglish. These are vendor-reported comparisons.\n- **Names and terms**: each request can include a custom vocabulary of up to **1,000** names or technical terms. A phonetic matcher shortlists entries that sound like what was said, and Sarvam 105B uses the full sentence to decide whether to apply a match.\n- **Latency**: in live dictation it transcribes and edits each phrase as the speaker finishes it; when speech ends, only the final phrase is left to process. Across sessions from 10 seconds to an hour, the company cites a median **0.65** seconds from the end of speech to the edited text (defer to the blog for the exact wording).\n- **Training**: the dictation 105B was trained with reinforcement learning (GRPO). For each Saaras V4 transcript the model wrote 16 answers, scored by plain code: 1 if every required edit was made and the meaning kept, 0 if any check failed, and −1 for harmful changes such as an altered number.\n- **Price**: recognition and editing ship in one call, at **₹0.50 per minute** of audio.\n- **Prior use**: the same stack has been running in Sarvam’s dictation app **Kivi**, where the company says people have dictated more than **30 million** words.\n\n## Relation to earlier Sarvam listings\n\nThis site already lists **Sarvam-105B-1711**, **Sarvam Prism**, and **Sarvam Token Factory**. Dictate is a product API that applies 105B to dictation editing, not a new base-model name.\n"
