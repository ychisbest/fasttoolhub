intro: "**ProtoMap** from **HoundDog.ai** is a **gRPC / Protobuf** context engine and model harness: it scans source into an org-wide graph, then serves engineers and coding agents over the **ProtoMap MCP** for call sites, blast radius, field lineage and more. On their synthetic ~5M LOC benchmark they report about **13× fewer tokens, 7.7× faster answers, and 6.4× lower cost** versus a bare agent (see their table; numbers move with models and repos).\n\nFlow: `protomap scan` → graph (repos, services, methods, fields, messages) → optional BYO-model false-positive prune → MCP query. Languages: Go, Java, Kotlin, C#, Python, TypeScript, JavaScript, Rust, Swift; mono- and multi-repo. Self-hosted via Docker + PostgreSQL (trial installer). They state SOC 2, regular pentests, and membership in Anthropic Defense Access and OpenAI Daybreak Blue. Roadmap mentions REST/OpenAPI, GraphQL and Thrift.\n\nHighlights:\n\n- Product https://protomap.ai/ , quickstart https://docs.protomap.ai/quickstart/ ;\n- Installer repo https://github.com/hounddogai/protomap ;\n- Works with Claude Code, Codex, Antigravity, VS Code, OpenCode and other MCP clients;\n- Analysis aimed at a modern laptop — no GPU required per their copy;\n- Best for large gRPC microservice codebases, not a universal code-search replacement."
explain: "## Why it matters\n\nCross-repo gRPC blast-radius work burns tokens and still misses indirect callers. ProtoMap builds a deterministic graph from code so agents stop rediscovering the same edges, and “who calls this RPC?” becomes one tool call. For platform teams already paying agent bills, that is a direct cost and accuracy lever; the Product Hunt launch makes trial easy.\n\n## Limits\n\nToday is gRPC/Protobuf-first; other protocols are forthcoming. Benchmarks are synthetic and model-specific. Self-host needs Docker Compose plus RAM/disk; repos without gRPC see little gain. Some review steps expect a BYO model."
