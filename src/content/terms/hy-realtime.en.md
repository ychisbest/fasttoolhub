intro: "**Hy Realtime** is the new real-time speech model released by Tencent Hunyuan on 13 November 2026. [Yicai](https://www.yicai.com/news/103440081.html) published at 16:38:55 Beijing time, and [National Business Daily](https://www.nbd.com.cn/articles/2026-11-13/4647109.html) at 16:46:38 wrote that the post came from Hunyuan’s official WeChat account. Both restated the official description: the model performs well on speech understanding, instruction following, and more human-like expression, and adds agentic tool use and singing. Hy Realtime is already live in Yuanbao voice calls and Tencent customer service, and Hunyuan said a Tencent Cloud API would follow soon.\n\n[IT Home](https://www.ithome.com/1/026/825.htm) (16:41:34) carried a longer official explainer. According to that account, the model can use conversation context to recover intent from mumbled, unfinished, or mid-sentence corrected input, and it keeps following multi-turn instructions in longer dialogues. Besides ordinary conversation, it can decide from context whether to run a web search, tying together the search decision, tool calls, and the spoken answer. Officials also said spoken delivery is better than the previous generation on audio quality, pronunciation, prosody, and emotion. The singing capability was co-designed with Tencent Music’s TME Lingyin AI team; users can request songs by artist, album, genre, language, release year, and popularity, or continue from earlier turns — for example, “play an English folk song from between 2010 and 2020.” The current version can be tried in the latest Yuanbao app by selecting the “初语” (Chuyu) voice in a voice call, or by opening the web demo at [rtcube.cloud.tencent.com](https://rtcube.cloud.tencent.com/component/hunyuan/index.html). Hunyuan said the model is still being iterated, with next steps including duplex interaction, longer-term personalized memory, and foreground/background agent interaction.\n\nThis page did not find a publicly reachable URL for the original WeChat post, so it relies on Yicai and National Business Daily’s restatement of that account, plus the official explainer quoted by IT Home.
