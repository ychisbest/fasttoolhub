It understands spoken requests, reasons over conversational context and produces **text responses plus structured tool calls**, while keeping text and image input.\n\nArchitecturally, a **Whisper large-v3-turbo** speech encoder is connected through a learned projector to a **Gemma 4 26B-A4B** backbone, so one shared model handles listening, reasoning and tool use.
