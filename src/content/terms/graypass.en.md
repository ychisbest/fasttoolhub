explain: "## Key points\n\n1. **The gap it fills**: authentication happens at login, but sessions stay valid long after. By the time a consequential action (a payment, a data export, an account change) arrives, the evidence behind that login has aged, and the actor may now be a different person or an AI agent.\n2. **Brainprint**: GrayPass's privacy-preserving, tenant-scoped model of how an enrolled person interacts with software: pointer movement and click timing, keystroke timing (not the words typed) and scrolling rhythm. It adds continuity evidence but doesn't establish identity or permission on its own.\n3. **Evidence it weighs**: identity, device, session and transaction context plus Brainprint continuity, measured against your policy for that specific action.\n4. **Action-bound proof**: authorization is tied to the exact action your application is about to execute.\n5. **Works with your stack**: you keep your identity provider, passkeys and MFA; GrayPass adds fresh assurance at the moment of action.\n6. **Traction claimed at launch**: 900K+ sessions processed and $50K in revenue that month. GrayPass also publishes research such as \"The Shape of Identity\" and \"The Chain of Intent\" on agency and delegation."
