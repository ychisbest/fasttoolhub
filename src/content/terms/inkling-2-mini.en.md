intro: "**Inkling 2 Mini** is an open-weights model that **Thinking Machines Lab** released on **20 November 2026**. It is a smaller member of the **Inkling 2** family, distinct from the already listed **Inkling 2 Small** (594B total parameters), and the lab says it is small enough to run on a laptop. Official announcement: [Introducing Inkling 2 Mini](https://thinkingmachines.ai/news/inkling-2-mini/). The official account [@thinkymachines](https://x.com/thinkymachines) posted at 2026-11-20T20:10:34Z (04:10 Beijing time on 21 November): [Some of you thought that Inkling 2 Small was too big…](https://x.com/thinkymachines/status/2123871643797995872).\n\nThe official page describes a **Mixture-of-Experts looped transformer** whose middle layers run three times per token, so the model can do more internal reasoning while writing fewer tokens. It has **26.4B total parameters** and **3.4B active** parameters per token, or about **5.4B** weight activations per token once looping is counted. The lab says it leads open-weights models with under 100B total parameters on **DeepSWE v1.1 (48.2%)** and **Vals Index v2.2 (46.5%)**; the official post also cites **20.1** on the Artificial Analysis Index. A **variable-loop** research variant is released alongside it for monitorability experiments. There is also a Hugging Face blog post: https://huggingface.co/blog/inkling2mini ."
explain: "## What problem it targets\n\nInkling 2 Small has 594B total parameters, which is still large for people who want a local setup or who want to study looping itself. Inkling 2 Mini puts a looped transformer in a 26B-class model so more people can run it on a laptop, and so the safety and monitorability community can study how loops affect chain-of-thought supervision.\n\n## Technical points from the official page\n\n- **Looped MoE**: the middle layers run three times per token, trading repeated compute for a shorter but still legible chain of thought;\n- **Scale**: 26.4B total parameters, 3.4B active, and about 5.4B weight activations per token;\n- **Training and serving**: the lab says most of the weights were trained and are deployed natively in **NVFP4**;\n- **Variable-loop research variant**: used to compare loop counts. The official write-up says the no-CoT time horizon improved from 16 seconds at one pass to 37 seconds with three; serial-reasoning performance kept improving out to ten loops, twice as many as it was trained with; the chain of thought grew shorter but stayed legible; and tests of agentic sabotage did not trend higher as loops were added.\n\n## Limits\n\nThe official comparisons are against open-weights models with under 100B total parameters, and evaluation setups can vary by model — the lab says so. This is not simply a shrunk copy of Inkling 2 Small: the two models differ by an order of magnitude in size and in intended use. This entry does not treat unofficial memory-footprint figures or third-party recaps as official numbers."
