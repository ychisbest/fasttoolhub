intro: "**TimeH2O-v1** is a **time-series foundation model** released by **H2O.ai** on **October 26, 2026**. With about **108 million parameters**, it forecasts **zero-shot**: it reads a series' history and predicts the future without being trained or fine-tuned on that data.\n\nAt launch it ranked **#1 of 32 models on the fev-bench leaderboard** (100 real-world forecasting tasks) by **win rate (91.9%)** and **skill score (50.8)**, ahead of Mistral's **Timestral-209M**, **t0-gamma**, Google's **TimesFM-3** and Amazon's **Chronos-2**. It also posts the lowest aggregate **MASE (0.654)** and **CRPS (0.441)** among zero-shot models on **GIFT-Eval**. The weights are on Hugging Face under the **H2O Model Non-Commercial License**, alongside a 5.8M-parameter **TimeH2O-v1-tiny**; H2O.ai kept promoting it on X through Halloween (\"Your forecasting model shouldn't take longer to train than it takes to become a skeleton\")."
explain: "## Model details\n\n- **Size**: ~108M parameters (TimeH2O-v1); **TimeH2O-v1-tiny** has 5.8M parameters for maximum throughput.\n- **Context**: up to **16,384 time steps**; **horizon** is unlimited.\n- **Output**: probabilistic forecasts as **9 quantiles** (0.1–0.9).\n- **Inputs**: univariate target series, with **optional covariates** (price, promotions, weather, etc.) — the tiny model does not support covariates.\n- **Architecture**: a purpose-built transformer with a **flip-equivariant** design (a flipped series gets the flipped forecast), trained on real-world series plus synthetic data generated with temporal causal models.\n\n## Benchmarks (as of Oct 23–24, 2026)\n\n| Benchmark | Metric | TimeH2O-v1 | Timestral-209M | TimesFM-3 | Chronos-2 |\n| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |\n| fev-bench | Skill score ↑ | 50.8 | 50.4 | 49.0 | 47.3 |\n| GIFT-Eval | MASE ↓ | 0.654 | 0.658 | 0.667 | 0.698 |\n| GIFT-Eval | CRPS ↓ | 0.441 | 0.446 | 0.456 | 0.485 |\n\nTimeH2O-v1 was never trained on any fev-bench dataset.\n\n## Licensing\n\nThe weights are **open but non-commercial**: internal benchmarking and testing are allowed, while production use requires a commercial license from H2O.ai. The `h2ofm` inference code is Apache-2.0. Access on Hugging Face is gated (accept the terms first).\n\n## Why it matters\n\nTime-series foundation models let companies replace hundreds of per-series models (demand, inventory, cash, energy load, patient volume) with one pretrained model. H2O.ai positions TimeH2O as the forecasting layer that AI agents can call for grounded numbers."
