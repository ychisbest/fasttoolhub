intro: "**Scribe v3** and **Scribe v3 Live** are ElevenLabs' new Speech to Text models, announced on 10 November 2026, for recorded audio and realtime audio respectively. In a [blog post](https://elevenlabs.io/blog/scribe-v3) by Min Kim, the company calls them its most accurate speech-to-text models yet, built for messy real-world audio: overlapping speakers, background noise, mid-sentence pauses, and language switching. ElevenLabs posted the announcement as [@ElevenLabs](https://x.com/ElevenLabs/status/2120217467100541396).\n\nAccording to the company, Scribe v3 makes 27% fewer word errors than Scribe v2 across 102 languages on an internal version of FLEURS, with improvements in every language tested, and 17% fewer diarization errors than v2 on recordings with three or more speakers — the lowest diarization error of any model ElevenLabs says it tested. Scribe v3 Live is the low-latency realtime variant: it reduces semantic word error rate by 43% and word error rate by 31% versus Scribe v2 Realtime, adds speaker diarization to realtime transcription for the first time, and uses the whole conversation (not just a silence timer) for turn detection, so a caller who pauses while reading a number is less likely to be cut off.\n\nScribe v3 Live is available in ElevenAgents today. Both models are in ElevenAPI — existing integrations switch by changing the model ID to `scribe_v3` or `scribe_v3_live` — and both are the default in the ElevenLabs app. The post also describes a Scribe v3 Live variant optimized for medical conversations in ElevenAgents. Default deployment is in the US, with isolated EU, India, and Singapore environments; Zero Retention Mode deletes audio and text when a request finishes. ElevenLabs says both models are covered by its SOC 2, ISO 27001, and PCI DSS Level 1 programs and are HIPAA-eligible with a BAA.\n\nHighlights:\n\n- Official blog (10 November 2026): https://elevenlabs.io/blog/scribe-v3 ;\n- Official announcement: https://x.com/ElevenLabs/status/2120217467100541396 ;\n- Product page: https://elevenlabs.io/speech-to-text ;\n- These names belong to the **Scribe** speech-to-text line. They are not the same product as **Eleven v4**, ElevenLabs' text-to-speech model."
