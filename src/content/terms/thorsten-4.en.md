intro: "**Thorsten-4** is the enterprise Large Psychology Model officially launched by the New York startup millionways on 12 November 2026. The official product page at [millionways.ai/thorsten](https://millionways.ai/thorsten) is titled “Thorsten-4 The Large Psychology Model for Enterprise AI,” and says it analyzes enterprise communication to understand how people and AI think, communicate, build trust, make decisions and perform: “While traditional AI understands language, Thorsten-4 understands behavior.”\n\n[Semafor reported](https://www.semafor.com/article/11/11/2026/new-ai-model-from-startup-millionways-wants-to-read-between-the-lines) at 9:00 a.m. EST on 12 November 2026 (14:00 UTC, 22:00 Asia/Shanghai) that millionways “on Thursday launched” the model, and that the company told Semafor it had raised $6 million in U.S. funding, with investors including football players Jason Kelce and DK Metcalf. The article says Thorsten-4 grew out of a research project in Germany in which dozens of psychologists interviewed more than 7,000 people about their personal lives and aspirations, then labeled the data by hand to capture what people’s words revealed about motives and thinking. The cofounders are Martin Cordsmeier and Max Weidemann. Weidemann told Semafor: “We can add a level of human understanding that the language models cannot get to by themselves,” adding that the model should be “a decision-making support tool rather than the ultimate decision-maker.” Semafor says the company sees it as particularly useful in sales and executive hiring — for example, prompting it on an interview transcript to gauge how a candidate will behave under pressure. At 15:24 UTC the same day, the official account [@millionwaysai](https://x.com/millionwaysai/status/2120900463684141345) pointed to the Semafor story; at 15:55 UTC it [posted](https://x.com/millionwaysai/status/2120908242843054394) that Thorsten-4 is a Large Psychology Model “built on 15+ years of human research.”\n\nAccording to the official product page, Thorsten-4 offers behavioral intelligence (motivation, decision styles, emotional regulation, communication dynamics, cognitive patterns and behavioral tendencies), multimodal analysis, enterprise-scale use on meetings, interviews, customer conversations, emails, documents, support interactions and AI conversations, and analysis of humans, AI agents and the interaction between them. Official deployment options are the millionways Platform, API, MCP or self-hosted infrastructure, to meet enterprise security and compliance needs. The page says every analysis includes behavioral measurements, behavioral factors, enterprise behavioral models for business outcomes, and recommended actions. It also lists official figures: more than 35 million behavioral observations, a 10-year research program, more than 100,000 hours of behavioral research, and 50+ behavioral scientists and psychologists. Those figures are the company’s statements; this page does not independently verify them. The company lists New York, NY 10016 and hello@millionways.ai.
